She Loves You: Inside The Beatles' Breakthrough 1963 Single

"She Loves You" is the song that turned Beatlemania from a British phenomenon into an unstoppable one.
Paul McCartney and John Lennon wrote it in a single day in June 1963, and it became the best-selling single of The Beatles' career in the UK.

How The Beatles Wrote "She Loves You" on Tour

"She Loves You" started on a tour bus on June 26, 1963, the night after a concert at the Majestic Ballroom in Newcastle upon Tyne.

Paul McCartney and John Lennon began the song that night in their Newcastle hotel room and finished it the next day at McCartney's family home on Forthlin Road in Liverpool.

Paul's Call-and-Response Idea That John Talked Him Out Of

McCartney's first idea was a strict call-and-response structure, modeled on Bobby Rydell's "Forget Him": one singer delivering the verse, the others answering "yeah yeah yeah."
Lennon pushed back, and the pair reworked it into a single voice reporting what a friend had said, a third-person device that was unusual for a pop love song at the time.

Recording "She Loves You" and George Harrison's Famous Chord

The Beatles recorded "She Loves You" in a single day, July 1, 1963, at EMI Studios in London, with producer George Martin.

The Major Sixth Ending George Martin Doubted

The song's closing chord, an unusual major sixth, was George Harrison's idea.
Martin questioned whether it would work on a pop single, but the band insisted, and the chord became one of the most recognizable endings in 1960s pop.

Why "She Loves You" Became The Beatles' Biggest UK Hit

"She Loves You" was released in the UK on August 23, 1963, backed with "I'll Get You," and entered the charts eight days later.

It reached number one on September 14, held the top spot for a month, dropped off, then returned to number one again on November 30, the day after it became the first Beatles single to sell over a million copies in Britain.

It remains The Beatles' best-selling single in the UK, and by April 1964 it helped the band hold all five of the top five spots on the US singles chart at once.

FAQ: She Loves You

When was She Loves You released?

She Loves You was released in the UK on August 23, 1963 and in the US on September 16, 1963, with "I'll Get You" as the B-side.

Who wrote She Loves You?

She Loves You was written by Paul McCartney and John Lennon, starting on a tour bus after a concert in Newcastle upon Tyne on June 26, 1963.
They finished it the next day at McCartney's family home in Liverpool.

Why does She Loves You end on a sixth chord?

The major sixth chord that closes She Loves You was George Harrison's idea.
Producer George Martin doubted it would work on a pop single, but the band kept it, and it became one of the song's signature touches.

How well did She Loves You perform on the charts?

She Loves You reached number one in the UK twice in 1963 and became the best-selling Beatles single in Britain.
In the US, it was part of a five-song sweep of the top five positions on the Billboard Hot 100 in April 1964.

"She Loves You" opened the door for the wave of British Invasion & Beat Music that followed, and it remains one of the defining early singles from The Beatles.
