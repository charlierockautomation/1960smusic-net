My Girl by The Temptations: The Song That Made David Ruffin a Star

By Charlie Gillingham · Published October 25, 2026 · Updated October 25, 2026

My Girl by The Temptations is the Smokey Robinson song that reached No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 on March 6, 1965.
Gordy Records released it on December 21, 1964.
It was the group's first US chart-topper, with David Ruffin on lead.

The Story Behind My Girl by The Temptations

An Answer to My Guy

Robinson conceived the song as a companion to "My Guy," the 1964 hit he wrote for Mary Wells, according to uDiscover Music.
It is believed his wife, Miracles member Claudette Robinson, inspired the lyrics.
Robinson later said it was "written with all the women in the world in mind," as quoted by Songfacts.
He wrote it with Ronald White, his bandmate in The Miracles.

A Song Meant for Someone Else

The Miracles were first in line to record it.
The Temptations persuaded Robinson to let them have it instead, and he rehearsed it with them for about a week.
He had a particular voice in mind: "I wanted to write something sweet for David Ruffin to sing," he said, as quoted by American Songwriter.
Ruffin had joined the group in January 1964, and Eddie Kendricks and Paul Williams had sung most earlier leads.
After this record, Ruffin became the main lead singer.

Recording at Hitsville

The Sessions

According to Wikipedia, the group cut the song at Hitsville U.S.A. in Detroit on September 25, November 10 and November 17, 1964.
Robinson and White produced it.
The single runs 2:56 and the album version runs 2:45.
The B-side is "(Talking 'Bout) Nobody But My Baby."

The Players

  • James Jamerson of the Funk Brothers plays the famous bass line that opens the record.
  • Robert White, a session guitarist and not the co-writer, plays the guitar riff.
  • Earl Van Dyke on piano and Benny Benjamin on drums.
  • Paul Riser arranged the horns and strings, with Detroit Symphony Orchestra players on the strings.

How It Charted

In the US

The record reached No. 1 on the Hot 100 in the chart week of March 6, 1965, knocking out Gary Lewis & the Playboys' "This Diamond Ring."
It was the first Hot 100 No. 1 for the Gordy label.
It also topped the R&B chart.
It appears on the album The Temptations Sing Smokey, which topped the R&B albums chart.

In the UK

It stalled at No. 43 on the UK Singles Chart at first.
A 1992 re-release, after the film My Girl, took it to No. 2.

The RIAA has certified it seven times Platinum in the US.

Legacy and Covers

Covers and Screen Time

Otis Redding recorded it in 1965 for Otis Blue, and his version reached No. 11 in the UK.
uDiscover counts more than 150 recordings in all.
Suave took a 1988 version to No. 20 on the Hot 100.
The Library of Congress added the original to the National Recording Registry in 2017.
The 1991 film My Girl, with Macaulay Culkin and Anna Chlumsky, took its title from the song.

Where It Fits

The single came out in late 1964 and climbed to the top in 1965, as the Motown boom that began in 1964 rolled on.

FAQ

Who wrote My Girl?

Smokey Robinson and Ronald White of The Miracles wrote it.
They also produced it.

Who sang lead on My Girl?

David Ruffin sang lead.
It was the first Temptations single with him out front.

When did My Girl come out?

Gordy Records released it on December 21, 1964.
Its B-side is "(Talking 'Bout) Nobody But My Baby," and it appears on the album The Temptations Sing Smokey.

Did My Girl hit No. 1?

Yes, it reached No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 on March 6, 1965.
It also topped the R&B chart, and it was the first Hot 100 No. 1 for The Temptations.

Who played on My Girl?

The Funk Brothers played the track: James Jamerson on bass, Robert White on guitar, Earl Van Dyke on piano and Benny Benjamin on drums.
Paul Riser arranged the strings and horns.
