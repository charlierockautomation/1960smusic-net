The Four Tops cut the first Motown session at Golden World

The vocals for Standing in the Shadows of Love were taped on October 19, 1966, in the first Motown session at the Golden World studio on Detroit's Davison Avenue, per Classic Motown.

Berry Gordy had bought Golden World weeks earlier and it soon became Studio B, and the clip below is the finished Motown single.

The Who play all of Tommy at the Electric Factory in Philadelphia

The Who played the Electric Factory in Philadelphia on October 19, 1969, with the late show running the full Tommy story from Overture to We're Not Gonna Take It, per the setlist record.

The set also had Young Man Blues and Summertime Blues up front and My Generation to close, and the clip below is Pinball Wizard from Tommy.

John Lennon and Yoko Ono appear in court on drug charges

A day after their arrest, John Lennon and Yoko Ono appeared at Marylebone Magistrates' Court in London for a hearing that lasted five minutes, per the Beatles Bible.

Sergeant Norman Pilcher read the charges, the couple were remanded on bail until November 28, and the clip below is Revolution, the 1968 Beatles single.

The Beatles work on Hello, Goodbye in Studio One

The Beatles recorded in Studio One at Abbey Road from 7pm to 3.30am, overdubbing lead guitar parts, lead and backing vocals, and handclaps onto the Hello, Goodbye coda, per the Beatles Bible.

The song was still called Hello Hello, and the single reached the UK on November 24, 1967, and the clip below is the finished record.

1965 in 1960s music: the full year guide

concert

Them play Paris for French television

Them, with Van Morrison on vocals, performed in Paris on October 19, 1965, in a show filmed for French TV, per Past Daily.

The footage has been released on DVD, and the clip below is Gloria, the Van Morrison song that became the band's signature.

industry

The Beatles try a fan club Christmas message at Marquee Studios

Between Rubber Soul sessions, the Beatles went to Marquee Studios in Soho on October 19, 1965, to record their fan club Christmas message from a script by press officer Tony Barrow, per the Beatles Bible.

None of it was used, they redid it on November 8, and the clip below is Drive My Car from the album they were making.

1964

1964 in 1960s music: the full year guide

concert

The Beatles play two shows at the ABC Cinema in Edinburgh

The Beatles played the ABC Cinema in Edinburgh at 6.30pm and 8.50pm, the first of three nights in Scotland, for a fee of £850, per the Beatles Bible.

Mary Wells was on the bill and the set ran 10 songs, and the clip below is Can't Buy Me Love, one of them.

release

Simon & Garfunkel release Wednesday Morning, 3 A.M.

Columbia released the duo's debut album, recorded in March 1964 and produced by Tom Wilson, on October 19, 1964, per Wikipedia.

It flopped at first, then returned in January 1966 after The Sound of Silence was overdubbed with electric instruments, and the clip below is the title track.

concert

The American Folk Blues Festival opens its UK dates at Croydon

Howlin' Wolf and the 1964 American Folk Blues Festival played Fairfield Halls in Croydon on October 19, 1964, before moving on to Bradford two days later, per Concert Archives.

The tour carried Mississippi and Chicago blues to British audiences, and the clip below is Howlin' Wolf's Smokestack Lightnin'.

1963 in 1960s music: the full year guide

concert

The Beatles overwhelm the Pavilion Gardens in Buxton

The Beatles played the Octagon in Buxton's Pavilion Gardens on October 19, 1963, their second and last show at the venue, per the Beatles Bible.

About 2,500 fans came, police lost control of the crowd and many fainted, and the clip below is She Loves You.

Jennifer Holliday is born in Houston

Singer and actor Jennifer Holliday was born in Houston, Texas, on October 19, 1960, per Wikipedia.

She won a Tony Award for playing Effie White in Dreamgirls and a Grammy for And I Am Telling You I'm Not Going, and the clip below is that song.
