Johnny Cash plays Folsom State Prison for the first time

Johnny Cash played Folsom State Prison in California on November 8, 1966, for about 1,800 inmates, with the Carter Family and the Statler Brothers on the bill, per the UC Davis Library.

He returned in January 1968 to record At Folsom Prison, and the clip below is Folsom Prison Blues from that live album.

The 5th Dimension top the Hot 100 with Wedding Bell Blues

The 5th Dimension's Wedding Bell Blues was the No. 1 single on the Billboard Hot 100 dated November 8, 1969, per Wikipedia's list of 1969 number ones.

The Rolling Stones play two shows at the Forum

The Rolling Stones played two shows at the Forum in Inglewood, California on November 8, 1969, with Ike & Tina Turner, B.B. King and Terry Reid on the bill, per Wikipedia.

The shows were part of the band's 1969 American tour, and the clip below is a live Midnight Rambler from the tour's Madison Square Garden shows.

The Grateful Dead play the Fillmore Auditorium

The Grateful Dead played the Fillmore Auditorium in San Francisco on November 8, 1969, a show later released as Dick's Picks Volume 16, per Wikipedia.

That album came out in March 2000, and the clip below is a 1969 Dark Star recorded at Fillmore West.

The Beatles record Think for Yourself at Abbey Road

The Beatles recorded George Harrison's Think for Yourself in Studio Two at EMI Studios in London on November 8, 1965, under the working title Won't Be There With You, per the Beatles Bible.

Paul McCartney overdubbed a second bass part through a Tone Bender fuzzbox, which Wikipedia describes it as the first bass recorded that way, and the clip below is the song.

The Beatles play two shows at the Liverpool Empire

The Beatles played two shows at the Empire Theatre in Liverpool on November 8, 1964, their first appearance in the city since December 1963, per the Beatles Bible.

The Paul McCartney Project lists the same ten-song set for both shows, including Twist and Shout and Long Tall Sally, and the clip below is Can't Buy Me Love, one of the songs in it.

The Beatles play the Ritz Cinema in Belfast

The Beatles played the Ritz Cinema in Belfast on November 8, 1963, with about 300 police on duty to keep the crowds from crushing the band, per the Paul McCartney Project.

The show was part of their autumn 1963 UK tour, and the clip below is She Loves You, which was in the tour's set.

Leif Garrett is born in Hollywood

Leif Garrett, born Leif Per Nervik, was born in Hollywood, California on November 8, 1961, per Wikipedia.

He became a 1970s teen idol, and the clip below is his recording of Runaround Sue.
