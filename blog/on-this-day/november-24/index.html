The Beatles release Hello, Goodbye in the UK

Parlophone released the Beatles' single Hello, Goodbye (R 5655), backed with I Am the Walrus, in the UK on November 24, 1967, per Wikipedia.

It spent seven weeks at No. 1 in the UK and three on the US Hot 100, and the clip below is the A-side.

The Who start a Tuesday residency at the Marquee

The Who began a Tuesday night residency at the Marquee Club in London on November 24, 1964, per Wikipedia.

It ran for sixteen weeks, broke the club's attendance records and built their reputation, and the clip below is I Can't Explain, their first single as the Who, released in January 1965.

culture

Paul McCartney's father Jim marries Angela Williams

Jim McCartney, father of Paul McCartney, married Angela Williams on November 24, 1964, per the Paul McCartney Project, eight years after the death of his first wife, Mary.

Angela said they took a taxi to Carrog in North Wales for the ceremony, per her ClassicBands interview, and the clip below is the Beatles' I Feel Fine, released three days later.

The Beatles play the ABC Cinema in Hull

The Beatles played the ABC Cinema in Hull, Yorkshire, on Sunday, November 24, 1963, their third performance in the city, per the Beatles Bible.

They were mid-tour and five days from releasing I Want to Hold Your Hand in the UK, and the clip below is that song.

The Four Seasons hold No. 1 with Big Girls Don't Cry

Big Girls Don't Cry by the Four Seasons was No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated November 24, 1962, the second of its five weeks on top, per Wikipedia.

It followed the Crystals' He's a Rebel at the top and held until Telstar took over, and the clip below is the Four Seasons single.
