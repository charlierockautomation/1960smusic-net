Diana Ross and the Supremes release Someday We'll Be Together

Motown issued Diana Ross and the Supremes' Someday We'll Be Together as M 1156, backed with He's My Sunny Boy, per Wikipedia.

It topped the Billboard Hot 100 on December 27, 1969, the last of the group's twelve US No. 1 singles and the final Hot 100 chart-topper of the decade.

The Beatles work on eight White Album tracks at Abbey Road

The Beatles spent the day in Studio Two at EMI Studios with George Martin and engineer Ken Scott, working on I Will, Birthday, Savoy Truffle, While My Guitar Gently Weeps, Long Long Long, Yer Blues, Sexy Sadie and What's the New Mary Jane, per the Beatles Bible.

Ringo Starr and his wife Maureen left for Sardinia the same day, with the White Album recording complete, and the clip below is Savoy Truffle, one of the tracks from the session.

Bobbie Gentry's Ode to Billie Joe knocks Sgt. Pepper off No. 1

Bobbie Gentry's debut album Ode to Billie Joe reached No. 1 on Billboard's Top LPs chart dated October 14, ending the Beatles' 15-week run with Sgt. Pepper, per Wikipedia.

The title single had already topped the Hot 100, and the clip below is that country-pop story-song.

The Easybeats release Friday on My Mind in the UK

United Artists issued the Easybeats' Friday on My Mind, backed with Made My Bed, Gonna Lie in It and produced by Shel Talmy, per Wikipedia.

The Vanda and Young song reached No. 6 in the UK and No. 16 on the US Hot 100 in May 1967.

The Beatles play two shows at the ABC Cinema in Manchester

The Beatles played Manchester's ABC Cinema in Ardwick at 6:30pm and 8:45pm, the fifth date of their autumn 1964 British tour, per the Beatles Bible.

Earlier that day they recorded an appearance for Granada's Scene at 6.30, and the ten-song set ended with Long Tall Sally, the clip below.

Cliff Richard releases 21 Today on his 21st birthday

Cliff Richard released the album 21 Today on Columbia on his 21st birthday, with the Shadows backing him on every track, per Wikipedia.

It became his first No. 1 on the UK albums chart, and the clip below is a British Pathe newsreel titled Cliff's 21 Today.

John F. Kennedy proposes the Peace Corps on the Michigan Union steps

Senator John F. Kennedy reached the University of Michigan around 2 a.m. and spoke for about three minutes to students waiting on the Michigan Union steps, asking how many would spend time working abroad, per the University of Michigan Record.

Within weeks 1,000 students had signed a petition for a Peace Corps, which was created by executive order on March 1, 1961, and the clip below is the Peace Corps' own video of the speech.
