The Rolling Stones' Cleveland show ends in a balcony fall and a ban

The Rolling Stones played Cleveland Public Hall on November 3, 1964, where fans rushed the stage, police stopped the show, and a 17-year-old girl fell from the balcony, per Cleveland Historical.

She was treated for bruises, and Mayor Ralph Locher then barred major rock acts from the city's public venues until summer 1966, and the clip below is Time Is on My Side, the band's first US top-ten hit.

The Beatles finish filming Magical Mystery Tour

The Beatles record Michelle at Abbey Road

The Beatles recorded Michelle in Studio Two at EMI Studios on November 3, 1965, with George Martin producing, per the Beatles Bible.

The Beatles' Drop In performance airs on Swedish TV

Swedish television first showed the Beatles' appearance on Drop In on November 3, 1963, taped on October 30 at the Arenateatern in Stockholm's Gröna Lund park, per the Beatles Bible.

They played I Saw Her Standing There and finished with Long Tall Sally, and the clip below is I Saw Her Standing There.

The Crystals hit No. 1 with He's a Rebel

The Crystals' He's a Rebel was No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated November 3, 1962, replacing Monster Mash, per Billboard.

Darlene Love and the Blossoms sang the record, which Phil Spector produced and Gene Pitney wrote, and the clip below is the single, a girl-group hit from the Brill Building era.

Elvis enters at UK No. 1 with It's Now or Never

Elvis Presley's It's Now or Never was the UK No. 1 single on the chart dated November 3, 1960, in its first week on the chart, per the Official Charts Company.
