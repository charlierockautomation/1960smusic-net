The Kinks released The Kinks Are the Village Green Preservation Society on Pye in the UK on November 22, 1968, the same day as the White Album, per Wikipedia.

It missed the charts on release but later became the band's best-selling UK album, and the clip below is the title track.

Bob Dylan married Sara Lownds in a private ceremony on Long Island, New York, on November 22, 1965, per Wikipedia.

The Beatles release With the Beatles in the UK

Parlophone released With the Beatles (PMC 1206) in the UK on November 22, 1963, eight months to the day after Please Please Me, per Wikipedia.

It had advance orders of half a million and spent 21 weeks at No. 1, and the clip below is All My Loving.

The Beatles play the Globe Cinema in Stockton-on-Tees

The Beatles played the Globe Cinema in Stockton-on-Tees on Friday, November 22, 1963, the 18th date of their autumn tour, per the Beatles Bible.

It was the day With the Beatles came out, and the clip below is Twist and Shout, the closer of their 10-song set.

Phil Spector releases A Christmas Gift for You

Philles Records released A Christmas Gift for You from Phil Spector in the US on November 22, 1963, with the Ronettes, the Crystals, Darlene Love and Bob B. Soxx and the Blue Jeans, per Wikipedia.

It sold poorly on the day of the Kennedy assassination but was named the greatest Christmas album by Rolling Stone in 2019, and the clip below is Darlene Love's Christmas (Baby Please Come Home).

President Kennedy is assassinated in Dallas

John F. Kennedy was shot in Dallas, Texas, on November 22, 1963, the same day With the Beatles and A Christmas Gift for You were released, per Wikipedia.

Dion's 1968 tribute Abraham, Martin and John names him, and the clip below is that song.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happened in 1960s music on November 22?

November 22 saw 12 verified music events across eight years, including the White Album and The Kinks Are the Village Green Preservation Society in 1968, With the Beatles in 1963, and Bob Dylan's wedding in 1965.

When was the White Album released?

The Beatles released the White Album in the UK on November 22, 1968, and in the US three days later, per Wikipedia. The Kinks released The Kinks Are the Village Green Preservation Society on the same day.

What came out on the day Kennedy was assassinated?

With the Beatles was released in the UK and A Christmas Gift for You from Phil Spector in the US on November 22, 1963. The Beatles also played the Globe Cinema in Stockton-on-Tees that day.

When did Bob Dylan marry Sara Lownds?

Bob Dylan married Sara Lownds in a private ceremony on Long Island, New York, on November 22, 1965, per Wikipedia. She was pregnant with their first child.

Did Come Together reach No. 1 on November 22, 1969?

No. Wedding Bell Blues by the 5th Dimension was No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated November 22, 1969. The Beatles' Come Together and Something took over on the chart dated November 29.

Did the Beatles start recording Strawberry Fields Forever on November 22, 1966?

No. The Beatles began recording Strawberry Fields Forever at EMI Studios in Abbey Road on November 24, 1966, per the Beatles Bible.
