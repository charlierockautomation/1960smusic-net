The Rolling Stones play the West Palm Beach festival

The Rolling Stones played the West Palm Beach International Music and Arts Festival at Palm Beach International Raceway on November 30, 1969, per Wikipedia.

The show was added to their American tour while it was already under way, and the clip below is Honky Tonk Women, their 1969 single.

The Jimi Hendrix Experience play Cobo Hall in Detroit

The Jimi Hendrix Experience played Cobo Hall in Detroit on November 30, 1968, with Cat Mother and the All Night Newsboys in support, per the official Jimi Hendrix encyclopedia.

Their double album Electric Ladyland had reached shops weeks earlier, and the clip below is their 1968 cover of Bob Dylan's All Along the Watchtower.

Bob Dylan records Can You Please Crawl Out Your Window?

Bob Dylan is generally dated to November 30, 1965 for the session in Columbia Studio A in New York that produced the single version of Can You Please Crawl Out Your Window?, per Wikipedia.

Some Dylan scholars argue for an earlier date, Columbia released the single on December 21, 1965, and the clip below is the mono single version.

Brian Epstein appears on Desert Island Discs

Brian Epstein's appearance on the BBC radio series Desert Island Discs was first broadcast on November 30, 1964, per the Beatles Bible.

The Beatles manager had been in the national spotlight for a year by then, and the clip below is A Hard Day's Night, the title song of their 1964 film.

The Beatles play the Empire Theatre in Sunderland

The Beatles played the Empire Theatre in Sunderland on Saturday, November 30, 1963, their second appearance at the venue after February 9, 1963, per the Beatles Bible.

It came a day after their Huddersfield dates and eight days after With the Beatles reached shops, and the clip below is It Won't Be Long, the album's opening track.

Paul McCartney and Pete Best are deported from Germany

Paul McCartney and Pete Best were deported from Germany on November 30, 1960, the day after Hamburg police arrested them on suspicion of arson, per the Beatles Bible.

The arrest followed a complaint about a fire at the Bambi Kino, and the clip below is My Bonnie, which the Beatles recorded in Hamburg with Tony Sheridan the following year.
