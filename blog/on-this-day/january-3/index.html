The Beatles spend day two of the Get Back sessions at Twickenham

The Beatles rehearsed at Twickenham Film Studios on January 3, 1969, the second day of the Get Back sessions, bringing Don't Let Me Down close to its final arrangement and working on I've Got a Feeling and Two of Us, per the Paul McCartney Project.

Paul McCartney also introduced Maxwell's Silver Hammer that day, and the clip below is I've Got a Feeling from the band's Topic channel.

Carl Wilson fails to report for Army induction

Beach Boys guitarist Carl Wilson did not report for military service on January 3, 1967, after receiving his draft notice, and refused to comply on religious grounds, according to American Songwriter.

A federal grand jury indicted him that April, and the clip below is God Only Knows, the Beach Boys song he sings lead on, from the band's official VEVO channel.

The Beatles play the Astoria Theatre for the ninth night

The Beatles played the Astoria Theatre in Finsbury Park, London, on January 3, 1964, the ninth concert there and part of The Beatles Christmas Show, per the Paul McCartney Project.

Their setlist included I Want to Hold Your Hand, and the clip below is that single from the band's Topic channel.

industry

The Rolling Stones cut Route 66 and Carol at Regent Sound

The Rolling Stones began recording their debut album at Regent Sound Studios in London on January 3, 1964, cutting Carol, Mona, Route 66, Walking the Dog and You Can Make It If You Try, according to Rolling Stones Data.

The album was recorded between January 3 and February 25 and released that April, and the clip below is Route 66 from the band's Topic channel.

The Beatles open a Scottish tour in Elgin

The Beatles played the Two Red Shoes ballroom in Elgin on January 3, 1963, the first date of their Winter 1963 Scotland tour, which went on to Dingwall and Bridge of Allan, per the Paul McCartney Project.

Their single Please Please Me followed on January 11, and the clip below is that song from the band's Topic channel.

1961 in 1960s music: the full year guide

industry

The Montgomery Brothers record Groove Yard

Wes Montgomery, his brothers Buddy and Monk, and drummer Bobby Thomas recorded the Riverside album Groove Yard at Plaza Sound Studios in New York on January 3, 1961, per the Riverside Records discography at Jazzdisco.

The session also produced Delirium and Doujie, and the clip below is the title track from the group's Topic channel.

industry

The Billy Taylor Trio records Interlude at Van Gelder Studio

Pianist Billy Taylor, bassist Doug Watkins and drummer Ray Mosca recorded nine tracks, opening with You Tempt Me, at Van Gelder Studio in Englewood Cliffs, New Jersey, on January 3, 1961, for the album Interlude on Moodsville, per the Van Gelder Studio discography at Jazzdisco.

The album was later reissued as Warming Up!, and the clip below is You Tempt Me from the trio's Topic channel, a track for fans of jazz and easy listening.

Bobby Darin and Connie Francis duet on The Ed Sullivan Show

Bobby Darin and Connie Francis shared the bill on The Ed Sullivan Show on January 3, 1960, and sang You Make Me Feel So Young and You're the Top together, per TheTVDB.

It was the first Sullivan broadcast of the new decade, and the clip below is the duet from the show's official channel.

Frequently Asked Questions

What did the Beatles work on at Twickenham on January 3, 1969?

On January 3, 1969, the second day of the Get Back sessions, the Beatles rehearsed at Twickenham Film Studios, bringing Don't Let Me Down close to its final arrangement and working on I've Got a Feeling and Two of Us. Paul McCartney also introduced Maxwell's Silver Hammer that day.

When did The Jack Paar Program show a Beatles clip?

The Jack Paar Program showed a film clip of the Beatles performing She Loves You on January 3, 1964, about five weeks before their live debut on The Ed Sullivan Show on February 9.

Where did the Beatles play on January 3, 1964?

On January 3, 1964, the Beatles played the Astoria Theatre in Finsbury Park, London, the ninth concert there, as part of The Beatles Christmas Show. Their setlist included I Want to Hold Your Hand.

When did the Rolling Stones begin recording their first album?

The Rolling Stones began recording their debut album at Regent Sound Studios in London on January 3, 1964, cutting Carol, Mona, Route 66, Walking the Dog and You Can Make It If You Try. The album was recorded between January 3 and February 25, 1964, and released in April.

Where did the Beatles open their first tour of 1963?

The Beatles opened their Winter 1963 Scotland tour at the Two Red Shoes ballroom in Elgin on January 3, 1963, then played Dingwall on January 4 and Bridge of Allan on January 5. Their single Please Please Me was released on January 11.

When did Carl Wilson fail to report for Army induction?

Carl Wilson of the Beach Boys did not report for military service on January 3, 1967, after receiving his draft notice, and refused to comply on religious grounds. A federal grand jury indicted him on April 5, 1967.

When did the Montgomery Brothers record Groove Yard?

Wes Montgomery, Buddy Montgomery, Monk Montgomery and drummer Bobby Thomas recorded the Riverside album Groove Yard at Plaza Sound Studios in New York on January 3, 1961. The session also produced Delirium and Doujie.

What did the Billy Taylor Trio record on January 3, 1961?

The Billy Taylor Trio, with Doug Watkins on bass and Ray Mosca on drums, recorded nine tracks for the Moodsville album Interlude at Van Gelder Studio in Englewood Cliffs, New Jersey, on January 3, 1961. The album was later reissued as Warming Up!.

Who appeared on The Ed Sullivan Show on January 3, 1960?

Bobby Darin and Connie Francis appeared on The Ed Sullivan Show on January 3, 1960, and sang You Make Me Feel So Young and You're the Top together. Edgar Bergen, Gene Krupa and Sal Mineo were also on the bill.
