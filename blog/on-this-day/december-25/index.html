The Dave Clark Five reach No. 1 with Over and Over

The Dave Clark Five's Over and Over was No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated December 25, 1965, in its seventh week on the chart after debuting at No. 63 on November 13.

It was the last No. 1 of 1965 and the group's only week on top in the United States, and the clip below is the single from the band's official catalog.

Paul McCartney and Jane Asher announce their engagement

Paul McCartney and the actress Jane Asher announced their engagement on Christmas Day 1967, after four years together, per the Beatles Bible.

Asher ended it publicly on the BBC in July 1968, and the clip below is the Beatles' Hello, Goodbye, which was No. 1 on the Hot 100 dated December 30, 1967.

James Brown holds No. 1 on the R&B chart with I Got You (I Feel Good)

James Brown and the Famous Flames' I Got You (I Feel Good) was No. 1 on Billboard's R&B singles chart dated December 25, 1965, its fourth week on top, ahead of the Packers' Hole in the Wall.

The single had entered the chart at No. 33 on November 13, and the clip below is the official visualizer from James Brown's Vevo channel.

Lou Christie's Lightnin' Strikes debuts on the Hot 100

Lou Christie's Lightnin' Strikes entered the Billboard Hot 100 dated December 25, 1965 at No. 93.

It reached No. 1 on the chart dated February 19, 1966, and the clip below is the original MGM single from Christie's official catalog.

The Young Rascals debut on the Hot 100 with I Ain't Gonna Eat Out My Heart Anymore

The Young Rascals' I Ain't Gonna Eat Out My Heart Anymore entered the Billboard Hot 100 dated December 25, 1965 at No. 94, one place below Lou Christie's entry.

It peaked at No. 52 on the chart dated February 5, 1966, and the clip below is the single version from the band's catalog.

The Beatles are Christmas Day guests on Saturday Club

The Beatles were the Christmas Day 1965 guests on the BBC Light Programme's Saturday Club, in an interview with Brian Matthew that was taped in three parts on November 29, per the Beatles Bible.

The programme aired from 10 to 11:30 a.m., and the clip below is We Can Work It Out, the double A-side with Day Tripper that the band had released in Britain on December 3.
