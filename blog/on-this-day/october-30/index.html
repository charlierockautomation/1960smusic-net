Marvin Gaye releases I Heard It Through the Grapevine

Tamla released Marvin Gaye's single I Heard It Through the Grapevine, backed with You're What's Happening (in the World Today), on October 30, 1968, per Wikipedia.

It reached No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 on the chart dated December 14, 1968, and stayed there for seven weeks, and the clip below is the single version.

Brian Jones is sentenced to nine months in prison

Brian Jones of the Rolling Stones admitted drug possession in court on October 30, 1967, and was sentenced to nine months in prison, per Songfacts.

A London appeals court overturned the sentence on December 12, 1967, and the clip below is the Stones' 1967 single We Love You.

The Beatles play two shows at the Gaumont in Bournemouth

The Beatles played two shows at the Gaumont Cinema in Bournemouth on October 30, 1964, on their autumn UK tour, per the Paul McCartney Project.

Both shows used the same ten-song set, and the clip below is A Hard Day's Night, which was on it.

1963 in 1960s music: the full year guide

The Beatles tape Drop In for Swedish television

The Beatles taped an appearance on the Swedish television show Drop In in Stockholm on October 30, 1963, the day after their last Swedish concert, per the Beatles Bible.

Producer Klas Burling talked them into one song beyond the planned She Loves You and Twist and Shout, and the clip below is She Loves You.
