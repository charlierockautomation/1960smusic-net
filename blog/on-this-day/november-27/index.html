Jimi Hendrix visited the Rolling Stones backstage at Madison Square Garden on his 27th birthday, November 27, 1969, and watched the show from behind Keith Richards' speaker stack, per Rolling Stones Data.

Mick Jagger went to a birthday party for Hendrix afterward, and the clip below is Hey Joe.

The Rolling Stones play their first Madison Square Garden shows

The Rolling Stones played their first shows at Madison Square Garden in New York on November 27, 1969, with the Maysles brothers filming for the movie Gimme Shelter, per Rolling Stones Data.

Ike & Tina Turner supported the tour, the shows fed the live album Get Yer Ya-Ya's Out!, and the clip below is Love in Vain.

culture

John Lennon explains returning his MBE on the radio

Radio reporter Ken Zelig interviewed John Lennon on November 27, 1969, two days after Lennon returned his MBE, per the Beatles Bible.

He gave the medal back on November 25, and the clip below is Cold Turkey, the single his letter said was slipping down the charts.

Capitol releases Magical Mystery Tour in the US

Capitol Records released the Beatles' Magical Mystery Tour LP in the US on November 27, 1967, pairing the six film songs with five 1967 singles, per Wikipedia.

It spent eight weeks at No. 1 on Billboard's Top LPs chart from the start of 1968, and the clip below is the title song.

Capitol releases Hello, Goodbye in the US

The Beatles' last US single of 1967, Hello, Goodbye, came out on November 27, 1967, three days after its UK release, per the Beatles Bible.

It followed the UK single by three days, and the clip below is the A-side.

Hendrix plays two shows in Belfast on his 25th birthday

The Jimi Hendrix Experience played two shows at Whitla Hall, Queen's College, Belfast, on November 27, 1967, as part of Festival '67, per a Belfast show archive.

The festival gave him a birthday cake before the shows, per This Day in Music, it was the band's only Irish date, and the clip below is Purple Haze.

John Lennon films a second Not Only... But Also appearance

John Lennon filmed his second appearance on the BBC comedy show Not Only... But Also on November 27, 1966, per the Beatles Bible.

He had written Strawberry Fields Forever in Spain that autumn, and the clip below is the finished song.

Herb Alpert's Whipped Cream & Other Delights hits No. 1

Herb Alpert & the Tijuana Brass were No. 1 on Billboard's album chart dated November 27, 1965, with Whipped Cream & Other Delights, per the Billboard chart archive.

It was the first week of the album's run on top, and the clip below is the title track.

The Rolling Stones play Dayton and Cincinnati

The Rolling Stones played two shows on November 27, 1965, at Wampler's Hara Arena in Dayton and the Gardens in Cincinnati, Ohio, per Rolling Stones Data.

They were deep into their 1965 North American tour, and the clip below is Get Off of My Cloud.

1964 in 1960s music: the full year guide

Parlophone releases I Feel Fine in the UK

Parlophone released the Beatles' standalone single I Feel Fine, their eighth for the label, in the UK on November 27, 1964, per the Beatles Bible.

It opens on a feedback note from a guitar, per Wikipedia, and the clip below is the A-side.

broadcast

Ready Steady Go! airs the Beatles' November performance

The Beatles' third and final Ready Steady Go! appearance was filmed at Wembley Studios on November 23, 1964, and edited into the ITV edition broadcast on November 27, per the Beatles Bible.

It aired on the day I Feel Fine came out, and the clip below is the B-side, She's a Woman.

Elvis Presley's Fun in Acapulco opens in the US

Paramount released Elvis Presley's film Fun in Acapulco, directed by Richard Thorpe and co-starring Ursula Andress, in the US on November 27, 1963, per Wikipedia.

The soundtrack's Bossa Nova Baby was a Top 10 Billboard hit, and the clip below is that song.

The Beatles play the Rialto Theatre in York

The Beatles played the Rialto Theatre in York on November 27, 1963, their final trip to the venue, per the Beatles Bible.

They were two days from releasing I Want to Hold Your Hand in the UK, and the clip below is that song.

The Rolling Stones play the Empress Ballroom in Wigan

The Rolling Stones played the Empress Ballroom in Wigan, England, on November 27, 1963, per Rolling Stones Data.

They were still a club and ballroom act, and the clip below is I Wanna Be Your Man, a song they took from the Beatles.

The Beatles record their first London BBC radio session

The Beatles recorded Love Me Do, P.S. I Love You and Twist and Shout for the BBC Light Programme's The Talent Spot at the BBC Paris Studio on Regent Street on November 27, 1962, per the Beatles Bible.

It aired on December 4, 1962, and the clip below is P.S. I Love You.

The Rolling Stones play the Ealing Jazz Club

The Rolling Stones played the Ealing Jazz Club in London on November 27, 1962, without Bill Wyman and Charlie Watts, per Rolling Stones Data.

It was seven months before their first single, and the clip below is Come On.

The Beatles play their 58th lunchtime show at the Cavern

The Beatles played their 58th lunchtime performance at the Cavern Club in Liverpool on November 27, 1961, per the Beatles Bible.
