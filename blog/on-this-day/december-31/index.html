Jimi Hendrix rings in 1970 with Band of Gypsys at the Fillmore East

Jimi Hendrix and his Band of Gypsys played two shows at the Fillmore East in New York on December 31, 1969, before a sold-out crowd of 2,639, with the Voices of East Harlem opening, according to the Official Jimi Hendrix Site.

The band played the same stage twice more on January 1, 1970, and the clip below is Machine Gun from the Fillmore East run, from Hendrix's Topic channel.

The Monkees reach No. 1 on the Hot 100 with I'm a Believer

The Monkees' I'm a Believer climbed from No. 3 to No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated December 31, 1966, after entering the chart at No. 44 on December 10.

Written by Neil Diamond, it stayed at the top for seven weeks, according to the Songfacts calendar, and the clip below is the song from the band's Topic channel.

The Beatles play the sixth night of Another Beatles Christmas Show

The Beatles played two shows at the Hammersmith Odeon in London on December 31, 1964, at 6:15 pm and 8:45 pm, the sixth night of Another Beatles Christmas Show, per the Paul McCartney Project.

Paul McCartney and George Harrison then went to EMI producer Norman Newell's New Year's Eve party in London, and the clip below is I Feel Fine, the band's current single, from their Topic channel.

The Beatles play the Astoria Theatre on New Year's Eve

The Beatles played the Astoria Theatre in Finsbury Park, London, on December 31, 1963, part of The Beatles Christmas Show, which ran from December 24 to January 11, per the Paul McCartney Project.

Their She Loves You had been released in the UK on August 23, 1963, and the clip below is that song from the band's Topic channel.

culture

Jerry Garcia and Bob Weir meet in a Palo Alto music store

Jerry Garcia was waiting for banjo pupils at Dana Morgan's music store in Palo Alto on New Year's Eve 1963 when 15-year-old Bob Weir heard him playing and walked in, according to Palo Alto History.

They jammed and decided to start a jug band, Mother McCree's Uptown Jug Champions, a step toward the Grateful Dead, and the clip below is Viola Lee Blues from the Dead's Topic channel.
