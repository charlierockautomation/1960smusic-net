The Beatles' Portsmouth show is postponed

The Beatles' show at the Portsmouth Guildhall on November 12, 1963, was postponed after Paul McCartney fell ill with gastric flu, per the Portsmouth Guildhall.

The show was rescheduled for December 3, and the clip below is She Loves You.
