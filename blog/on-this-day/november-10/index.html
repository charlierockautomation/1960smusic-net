Jefferson Airplane play Crown of Creation on the Smothers Brothers

CBS aired the Smothers Brothers Comedy Hour episode with Jefferson Airplane on November 10, 1968, five days after Richard Nixon won the presidential election, per Patrick Burke in Popular Music.

The band played the title track of its fourth album, taped in late October, and Grace Slick ended it in dark makeup with a Black Power salute, which made the performance controversial, and the clip below is the song.

The Beatles film three Hello, Goodbye promo clips at the Saville Theatre

The Beatles arrived at the Saville Theatre in London on Friday, November 10, 1967, to make three promotional films for Hello, Goodbye, per the Beatles Bible.

A Musicians' Union ban on miming kept the clips off UK television, and the clip below is the song.

1965 in 1960s music: the full year guide

The Beatles record The Word at Abbey Road

The Beatles began a 9pm session in Studio Two at EMI, Abbey Road on November 10, 1965, cutting the rhythm track for The Word in three takes and a new backing track for I'm Looking Through You, per the Beatles Bible.

The session ran until 4am, after George Martin made stereo mixes of Run For Your Life and We Can Work It Out that afternoon, and the clip below is The Word from Rubber Soul.

1963 in 1960s music: the full year guide

The Beatles play the Hippodrome in Birmingham

The Beatles played the Hippodrome in Birmingham on Sunday, November 10, 1963, the ninth date of their autumn tour, per the Beatles Bible.

It was their second show at the venue, after one on March 10, 1963, and the clip below is She Loves You.

1962

1962 in 1960s music: the full year guide

chart

The Crystals hold No. 1 with He's a Rebel

The Crystals' He's a Rebel was the No. 1 single on the Billboard Hot 100 dated November 10, 1962, its second of two weeks at the top, per Wikipedia.

It first reached No. 1 on November 3 before Big Girls Don't Cry by the Four Seasons took over on November 17, and the clip below is the song.
