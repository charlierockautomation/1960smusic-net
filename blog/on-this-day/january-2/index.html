Led Zeppelin open at the Whisky a Go Go with Alice Cooper's band

Led Zeppelin played the first night of a Los Angeles run at the Whisky a Go Go on January 2, 1969, with the young Alice Cooper band on the bill, according to Alice Cooper's official site.

It was only the band's fifth show in the United States, ten days before the debut album's US release on January 12, and the clip below is the album opener Good Times Bad Times from the band's official channel.

Police seize the Two Virgins shipment at Newark

More than 30,000 copies of John Lennon and Yoko Ono's Unfinished Music No. 1: Two Virgins were seized by police at Newark Airport on January 2, 1969, over the nude cover photograph, according to Ultimate Classic Rock.

The album had come out the previous November in a plain brown wrapper and reached only No. 124 on the Billboard 200, and the clip below is Side One from Lennon's Topic channel.

The Beatles play two more shows at the Hammersmith Odeon

The Beatles played two shows at the Hammersmith Odeon in London on January 2, 1965, at 6:15 pm and 8:45 pm, the eighth night of Another Beatles Christmas Show, per the Paul McCartney Project.

The Beatles hold No. 1 with I Feel Fine for a second week

The Beatles' I Feel Fine stayed at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated January 2, 1965 for a second week, after entering the chart at No. 22 on December 5.

Come See About Me by the Supremes, already a two-week No. 1, held No. 2, and the clip below is the Beatles single from their official VEVO channel.

Petula Clark leaps from No. 41 to No. 12 with Downtown

Petula Clark's Downtown jumped from No. 41 to No. 12 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated January 2, 1965, after entering the chart at No. 87 on December 19.

It was only the song's third week on the chart, and the clip below is the single from her official channel.

The Righteous Brothers leap to No. 14 with You've Lost That Lovin' Feelin'

The Righteous Brothers' You've Lost That Lovin' Feelin' jumped from No. 34 to No. 14 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated January 2, 1965, after entering the chart at No. 77 on December 12.

It was in its fourth week on the chart, and the clip below is the single from the duo's Topic channel.

Elvis Presley hits No. 1 on the albums chart with Roustabout

Elvis Presley's Roustabout soundtrack rose from No. 2 to No. 1 on the Billboard album chart dated January 2, 1965, displacing The Beach Boys In Concert.

The Beach Boys' live album had spent four weeks at the top, and the clip below is the title song from Presley's Topic channel.
