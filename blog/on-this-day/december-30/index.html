Frank Sinatra records My Way in Hollywood

Frank Sinatra recorded My Way on December 30, 1968, at Western Recorders in Hollywood, with Don Costa arranging, according to the album's Wikipedia entry and the Songfacts calendar.

Songwriter and producer Bert Berns dies in New York

Bert Berns, the writer of Twist and Shout, Hang On Sloopy and Piece of My Heart, died of a heart attack on December 30, 1967, according to the Songwriters Hall of Fame.

His version of Piece of My Heart with Erma Franklin was rising on the charts when he died, and the clip below is the Isley Brothers' 1962 recording of Twist and Shout, which he produced, from the group's Topic channel.

The Rolling Stones debut on the Hot 100 with She's a Rainbow

The Rolling Stones' She's a Rainbow entered the Billboard Hot 100 dated December 30, 1967 at No. 77.

The song came from the band's album Their Satanic Majesties Request, and the clip below is the track from the ABKCO Vevo channel.

Frankie Valli debuts on the Hot 100 with To Give (The Reason I Live)

The Beatles play the fifth night of Another Beatles Christmas Show

The Beatles played two shows at the Hammersmith Odeon in London on December 30, 1964, at 6:15 pm and 8:45 pm, the fifth night of Another Beatles Christmas Show, per the Paul McCartney Project.

The show mixed comedy sketches with acts from Brian Epstein's roster before a short Beatles set that included Can't Buy Me Love, and the clip below is that song from the band's Topic channel.

1963 in 1960s music: the full year guide

concert

The Beatles play the Astoria Theatre in Finsbury Park

The Beatles played the Astoria Theatre in Finsbury Park, London, on December 30, 1963, part of The Beatles Christmas Show, which ran from December 24 to January 11, per the Paul McCartney Project.

Their I Want to Hold Your Hand had been released in the UK on November 29, and the clip below is that song from the band's Topic channel.

The Beatles play the twelfth night of their last Star-Club residency

The Beatles played the Star-Club in Hamburg on December 30, 1962, the twelfth date of their third and final residency there, per the Paul McCartney Project.

Their only single out at the time was Love Me Do, released in the UK on October 5, 1962, and the clip below is that song from the band's Topic channel.

The Beatles play an evening show at the Cavern Club

The Beatles played an evening show at the Cavern Club in Liverpool on December 30, 1961, per the Paul McCartney Project, two days before their Decca audition on January 1, 1962.

Their only record at the time was My Bonnie, made in Hamburg with Tony Sheridan, and the clip below is that song from the band's Topic channel.
