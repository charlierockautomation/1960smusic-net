Marvin Gaye holds No. 1 with I Heard It Through the Grapevine

Marvin Gaye's I Heard It Through the Grapevine was No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated December 21, 1968, its second week on top.

The Motown single stayed at No. 1 into 1969, and the clip below is Gaye's official lyric video.

chart

Glen Campbell's Wichita Lineman tops the album chart

Glen Campbell's Wichita Lineman was the No. 1 album on the Billboard Top LPs chart dated December 21, 1968, replacing Big Brother and the Holding Company's Cheap Thrills.

The Beatles' White Album took over a week later, and the clip below is the Jimmy Webb title song, a country crossover hit.

Janis Joplin debuts her new band in Memphis

Janis Joplin debuted her new band at the second annual Stax-Volt Yuletide Thing in Memphis on December 21, 1968, per the Songfacts timeline.

Keyboardist Bill King's first-hand account recalls a rough night at the Mid-South Coliseum on a Stax bill, and the clip below is Try (Just a Little Bit Harder) from the band's 1969 album.

The Beatles throw a Magical Mystery Tour fancy-dress party

The Beatles hosted a fancy-dress party at the Royal Lancaster Hotel in London on December 21, 1967, to celebrate finishing their Magical Mystery Tour TV film, per the Paul McCartney Project.

Paul McCartney and Jane Asher came dressed as a Pearly King and Queen, the film aired on BBC1 five days later, and the clip below is its title song.

Clarinets join When I'm Sixty-Four as Strawberry Fields Forever grows

At EMI Studios on December 21, 1966, three clarinettists added George Martin's score to the Beatles' When I'm Sixty-Four in a two-hour session, per the Paul McCartney Project.

That same day John Lennon added a vocal and Paul McCartney piano to the psychedelic Strawberry Fields Forever, and the clip below is the Beatles' official film for that song.

The Beatles start rehearsing Another Beatles Christmas Show

The Beatles began three days of rehearsals at the Hammersmith Odeon in London on December 21, 1964, for Another Beatles Christmas Show, which opened on December 24, per the Paul McCartney Project.

The Yardbirds were on the bill, I Feel Fine was No. 1 on the UK chart dated December 17, and the clip below is that single.

The Singing Nun holds No. 1 in America with Dominique

The Singing Nun's Dominique was No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated December 21, 1963, its third of four weeks on top.

Sung in French by Belgian nun Jeanine Deckers, it was one of the most unlikely No. 1s of the decade, and the clip below is the original recording.

broadcast

The Beatles' Christmas Saturday Club session airs on the BBC

BBC radio's Saturday Club broadcast a Beatles session on December 21, 1963, taped four days earlier at London's Playhouse Theatre, per the Paul McCartney Project.

The set included I Want to Hold Your Hand and This Boy, and the clip below is This Boy from that very broadcast, issued on On Air: Live at the BBC Volume 2.

concert

The Beatles preview their Christmas Show in Bradford

The Beatles played the Gaumont Cinema in Bradford on December 21, 1963, a preview of The Beatles Christmas Show with Cilla Black, Billy J. Kramer with the Dakotas, the Fourmost and Tommy Quickly, per the Paul McCartney Project.

A second preview followed in Liverpool the next night before the London run began on December 24, and the clip below is I Want to Hold Your Hand, No. 1 on the UK chart dated December 19.

The Beatles play night four of their last Star-Club run

The Beatles played the Star-Club in Hamburg on December 21, 1962, the fourth night of their final residency there, per the Paul McCartney Project.
