Joan Baez is arrested at the Oakland Induction Center

Joan Baez was arrested during Stop the Draft Week for sitting in at the entrance of the Armed Forces Induction Center in Oakland, California, and was sentenced to 10 days in jail, per Fredsakademiet.

It tied the folk movement to open resistance against the Vietnam War draft, and the clip below is We Shall Overcome, the protest song she carried onto the biggest stages of the decade.

The Beatles record Day Tripper at Abbey Road

The Beatles cut Day Tripper in Studio Two at EMI Studios from 2:30pm to midnight with George Martin and engineer Norman Smith, finishing only the third of three takes before adding vocals, tambourine and guitar overdubs, per the Beatles Bible.

Released on December 3 as a double A-side with We Can Work It Out, it hit No. 1 in the UK and No. 5 in the US, and the clip below is the finished record, built on a riff John Lennon called his own.

The Rolling Stones play two shows at the ABC in Northampton

The Rolling Stones played two shows at the ABC Theatre in Northampton on their second British tour of 1965, which ran from September 24 to October 17, per Wikipedia.

The tour closed the next night at the Granada in London, and the clip below is (I Can't Get No) Satisfaction, a song from their set that year.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happened in 1960s music on October 16?

October 16 saw 11 verified music events across the decade, including Patsy Cline's Crazy released in 1961, the Beatles recording Day Tripper in 1965, and Jimi Hendrix's Electric Ladyland released in the US in 1968.

When was Patsy Cline's Crazy released?

Decca released Patsy Cline's Crazy, written by Willie Nelson and produced by Owen Bradley, on October 16, 1961, and it reached No. 2 on the country chart and No. 9 on the Hot 100.

When did the Beatles record Day Tripper?

The Beatles recorded Day Tripper at EMI Studios in Abbey Road on October 16, 1965, in a session from 2:30pm to midnight that also began If I Needed Someone.

When was Electric Ladyland released in the US?

Reprise Records released Electric Ladyland by the Jimi Hendrix Experience in the United States on October 16, 1968, and it reached No. 1 on the Billboard chart by mid-November.

What was the Beatles' 24-hour White Album session?

On October 16, 1968 the Beatles began a session that ran from 5pm until 5pm the next day to mix, edit and sequence the White Album, with John Lennon, Paul McCartney and George Martin in charge while George Harrison and Ringo Starr were away.
