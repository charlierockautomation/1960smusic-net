BBC Television airs the first episode of Doctor Who

The Rolling Stones make their last Ed Sullivan Show appearance

The Rolling Stones played Gimme Shelter, Love in Vain and Honky Tonk Women on The Ed Sullivan Show, broadcast on November 23, 1969, per Wikipedia.

It was their last appearance on the show, the band mimed to prerecorded tracks while Mick Jagger sang live, and the clip below is Gimme Shelter.

Hey Jude spends its ninth week at No. 1

The Beatles' Hey Jude held No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 in the chart dated November 23, 1968, its ninth and final week on top, per Wikipedia.

Love Child by Diana Ross and the Supremes replaced it the following week, and the clip below is Hey Jude.

culture

Rolling Stone puts John Lennon and Yoko Ono on its cover

Rolling Stone issue 22, dated November 23, 1968, put John Lennon and Yoko Ono on its cover, per Wikipedia.

The cover showed the couple nude from behind, and the clip below is Revolution, the Lennon song the Beatles released as the B-side of Hey Jude.

The Jimi Hendrix Experience headline Cardiff

The Jimi Hendrix Experience played the Sophia Gardens Pavilion in Cardiff on November 23, 1967, on a UK package tour with Pink Floyd, The Move, The Nice and Amen Corner.

The tour ran two shows a night, and the clip below is Purple Haze.

Leonard Bernstein teaches modes with a Beatles song on CBS

CBS broadcast Leonard Bernstein's Young People's Concert What Is a Mode? on November 23, 1966, per Bernstein's website.

While explaining musical modes he asked viewers whether they knew the Beatles tune Norwegian Wood, and the clip below is the song.

The Beatles film ten promotional clips at Twickenham

The Beatles filmed ten promotional films at Twickenham Film Studios on November 23, 1965, directed by Joe McGrath, per the Beatles Bible.

They covered We Can Work It Out, Day Tripper, Help!, Ticket to Ride and I Feel Fine, and the clip below is the official video for We Can Work It Out.

1964 in 1960s music: the full year guide

Capitol releases The Beatles' Story double album

Capitol Records released the documentary double album The Beatles' Story in the US on November 23, 1964, per Wikipedia.

It mixed interviews, press conferences and song excerpts with narration, reached No. 7 on the Billboard Top LPs chart and went gold on December 31, 1964, and the clip below is I Want to Hold Your Hand on The Ed Sullivan Show.

release

Capitol releases I Feel Fine in the US

Capitol Records released the Beatles' I Feel Fine, backed with She's a Woman, in the US on November 23, 1964, four days before the UK release, per the Beatles Bible.

The record opens with a single feedback note, it spent three weeks at No. 1 on the Hot 100, and the clip below is I Feel Fine.

broadcast

The Beatles record their last Ready, Steady, Go! performance

The Beatles recorded their third and final performance for the pop show Ready, Steady, Go! at Wembley on November 23, 1964, per the Beatles Bible.

The songs they played are not listed in the Beatles Bible entry, so the clip below is Can't Buy Me Love, one of their 1964 number ones.

The Beatles play City Hall in Newcastle

The Beatles played the City Hall in Newcastle on Saturday, November 23, 1963, the 19th date of their autumn tour and their third visit to the venue, per the Beatles Bible.

They had played Newcastle in March and June that year, and the clip below is She Loves You.

1962 in 1960s music: the full year guide

culture

Time puts Joan Baez on its cover

Time magazine put 21-year-old folk singer Joan Baez on its cover on November 23, 1962, with a story titled Folk Singing: Sibyl with Guitar, per Time's archive.

The cover was a painting by Russell Hoban, and the clip below is Silver Dagger from her 1960 debut album.

industry

The Beatles audition for BBC television in London

The Beatles played a 10-minute lunchtime audition for a panel of BBC television producers at St James's Church Hall in London on November 23, 1962, per the Beatles Bible.

The Beatles Bible calls it evidently not a success, they did not make their BBC television debut until April 13, 1963, and the clip below is Love Me Do.

concert

The Beatles play the Tower Ballroom in New Brighton

The Beatles hurried back to Merseyside after the BBC audition and played the Tower Ballroom in New Brighton, Wallasey, on November 23, 1962, their 24th show there, per the Beatles Bible.

Love Me Do was a few weeks old, and the clip below is its B-side, P.S. I Love You.

G.I. Blues reaches US theaters nationwide

Paramount's G.I. Blues, Elvis Presley's first film after leaving the Army, went into wide US release on November 23, 1960, after opening in New York on November 4, per The Numbers and Wikipedia.

The film featured Wooden Heart, and the clip below is the song.

RCA Victor released the mono edition of Elvis Presley's gospel album His Hand in Mine (LPM-2328) on November 23, 1960, per Elvis Records.

He recorded it at RCA Studio B in Nashville on October 30 and 31, per Wikipedia, and the clip below is the title track.
