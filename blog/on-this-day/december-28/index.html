The Beatles' White Album reaches No. 1 on the Billboard 200

The Beatles' double album The Beatles, known as the White Album, moved from No. 2 to No. 1 on the Billboard 200 dated December 28, 1968, its first week on top after entering the chart at No. 11 on December 14.

The two-LP set had been released in November, and the clip below is Back in the U.S.S.R., the album's opening track, from the band's Topic channel.

The Miami Pop Festival opens at Gulfstream Park

The Miami Pop Festival opened at Gulfstream Park in Hallandale, Florida, on December 28, 1968, the first of three days that ran through December 30, per setlist.fm.

About 100,000 fans came over the weekend to two stages for what became known as the first major rock festival on the US East Coast, and the clip below is Both Sides Now by Joni Mitchell, one of the performers on the bill.
