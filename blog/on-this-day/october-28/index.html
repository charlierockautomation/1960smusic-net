Filming begins on the T.A.M.I. Show

Filming began on October 28, 1964, for the concert film T.A.M.I. Show at the Santa Monica Civic Auditorium in California, using the Electronovision video process, with a second night on October 29, per Wikipedia.

The bill included James Brown and the Famous Flames, the Rolling Stones, the Beach Boys, the Supremes, Chuck Berry and Marvin Gaye, and the clip below is Brown performing Please Please Please.

The Guess Who's Laughing is certified Gold

The Guess Who's single Laughing was certified Gold by the RIAA on October 28, 1969, after reaching No. 10 on the Billboard Hot 100, per the RIAA database.

It was the band's second US gold record after These Eyes, and the clip below is the song.

concert

The Miles Davis Quintet plays Antwerp

The Miles Davis Quintet of Davis, Wayne Shorter, Herbie Hancock, Ron Carter and Tony Williams played the Koningin Elisabethzaal in Antwerp, Belgium, on October 28, 1967, in a concert recorded by Belgian Radio and Television, per Sony Music.

The recording became the first disc of the 2011 box set Live in Europe 1967: The Bootleg Series Vol. 1, and the clip below is Footprints from that night.

EMI engineers mix We Can Work It Out in mono

EMI engineers made a rough mono mix of the Beatles' We Can Work It Out at Abbey Road on October 28, 1965, per the Beatles Bible.

The mix served as a playback track for a TV special filmed in Manchester on November 1 and 2, the band added more vocals the next day, and the clip below is the finished single.

The Beach Boys release Be True to Your School

Capitol Records released the Beach Boys' single Be True to Your School, backed with In My Room, on October 28, 1963, per Wikipedia.

It reached No. 6 on the Billboard Hot 100 and also appeared on the album Little Deuce Coupe, and the clip below is the song.

Louis Prima and Gia Maione play The Ed Sullivan Show

Louis Prima, Gia Maione and Sam Butera and the Witnesses performed Oh Marie and I Want You To Be My Baby on The Ed Sullivan Show on October 28, 1962, per AllMusic.

Maione, who joined Prima's act that year after a Mother's Day audition, also sang How High the Moon, and the clip below is Oh Marie from that broadcast.

Raymond Jones asks Brian Epstein for My Bonnie

Raymond Jones, a customer at Brian Epstein's NEMS record shop in Whitechapel, Liverpool, asked for the Beatles' single My Bonnie on October 28, 1961, per the Beatles Bible.

Epstein wrote that he noted the request, he first visited the Cavern Club on November 9, 1961, and the clip below is My Bonnie.

Sviatoslav Richter plays Carnegie Hall

Pianist Sviatoslav Richter played a recital at Carnegie Hall in New York on October 28, 1960, during his first American tour, per a Naxos Music Library listing of the recording made in performance.

The clip below is Beethoven's Piano Sonata No. 3 from his Carnegie Hall 1960 archive release.
