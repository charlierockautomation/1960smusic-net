The Jimi Hendrix Experience open a UK tour at the Royal Albert Hall

The Jimi Hendrix Experience opened a 16-date UK package tour at the Royal Albert Hall in London on November 14, 1967, with Pink Floyd, The Move, Amen Corner and The Nice on the tour bill, per the official Hendrix site.

Pink Floyd got a 17-minute slot built around Interstellar Overdrive, the Experience closed the bill with 40 minutes that opened on Foxey Lady, and the clip below is Hey Joe, which they played that night.

The Rolling Stones play two shows at Auburn

The Rolling Stones played two shows at the Memorial Coliseum in Auburn, Alabama, on November 14, 1969, the night after Dallas and before Champaign, per the Wikipedia tour page.

The Rolling Stones Data chronology also lists the Auburn date, and the clip below is Honky Tonk Women, their 1969 single.

The Archies' Sugar, Sugar was at No. 1 on the Official Singles Chart for 9 to 15 November 1969, per Official Charts.

The Rolling Stones play Knoxville

The Rolling Stones played the Knoxville Civic Auditorium and Coliseum in Tennessee on November 14, 1965, the night after Washington and before Charlotte, per the Wikipedia tour page.

The tour ran from October 29 to December 5 behind Out of Our Heads, and the clip below is Get Off Of My Cloud, the single released that autumn.

The Beatles tape Thank Your Lucky Stars at Teddington

The Beatles taped four songs for ITV's Thank Your Lucky Stars at Teddington Studios in London on November 14, 1964, per the Paul McCartney Project.

The set was I Feel Fine, She's a Woman, I'm a Loser and Rock and Roll Music, it aired on November 21, and the clip below is I Feel Fine.

The Beatles play the ABC Cinema in Exeter

The Beatles played the ABC Cinema in Exeter on Thursday, November 14, 1963, the night after Plymouth and before Bristol, per the Paul McCartney Project.

It was the second of three Beatles shows at that cinema and part of the autumn tour, and the clip below is Twist and Shout.

The Beatles play the Star-Club in Hamburg

The Beatles played the Star-Club in Hamburg on Wednesday, November 14, 1962, their 62nd concert at the club, during their second residency there, per the Paul McCartney Project.

Their next listed show was in Coventry on November 17, and the clip below is P.S. I Love You, the B-side of their debut single Love Me Do.

The Beatles play the Merseyside Civil Service Club

The Beatles played an evening show at the Merseyside Civil Service Club in Liverpool on Tuesday, November 14, 1961, their third concert at that venue, per the Paul McCartney Project.

Brian Epstein had first seen them at the Cavern five days earlier, and the clip below is My Bonnie.

Ray Charles hits No. 1 with Georgia on My Mind

Ray Charles reached No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated November 14, 1960, with Georgia on My Mind, per Stereogum.

It stayed at the top for one week, was the first of his three No. 1 hits, and the clip below is the song.
