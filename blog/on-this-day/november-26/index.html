They finished the B-side Ask Me Why in six takes, and the clip below is Please Please Me.

The Rolling Stones played the Civic Center in Baltimore, Maryland, on November 26, 1969, a stop on their 1969 American tour, per Wikipedia.

Cream play their farewell concert at the Royal Albert Hall

Cream played the second of two farewell nights at London's Royal Albert Hall on November 26, 1968, with Yes and Taste on the bill, per Wikipedia.

Hendrix and Pink Floyd play two shows at Manchester's Palace Theatre

The Jimi Hendrix Experience and Pink Floyd played two shows at the Palace Theatre in Manchester on November 26, 1967, the tenth date of a 16-date UK package tour, per This Day in Music.

The Beatles' Hello, Goodbye film airs on The Ed Sullivan Show

A promo film for the Beatles' Hello, Goodbye aired in the US on The Ed Sullivan Show on November 26, 1967, per the Paul McCartney Project.

The UK Musicians' Union ban on miming kept the film off British television, and the clip below is the song.

John Stirratt is born

John Stirratt, later the bassist of Wilco, was born on November 26, 1967, per Wikipedia.

The Supremes hold No. 1 with You Keep Me Hangin' On

The Supremes' You Keep Me Hangin' On was No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated November 26, 1966, its second week on top, per Wikipedia.

Arlo Guthrie is arrested for littering

Arlo Guthrie, then 18, was arrested for illegally dumping garbage in Massachusetts on November 26, 1965, the day after Thanksgiving, per Wikipedia.

He was convicted two days later, per Mass Moments, and the case became the story of Alice's Restaurant Massacree, the clip below.

The BBC airs The Beatles' Top Gear session

The BBC Light Programme broadcast the Beatles' Top Gear session on November 26, 1964, after they taped it on November 17 at the Playhouse Theatre in London, per the Beatles Bible.
