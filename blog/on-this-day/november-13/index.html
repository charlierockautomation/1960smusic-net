The Rolling Stones release Little Red Rooster

The Rolling Stones released Little Red Rooster in the UK on Friday, November 13, 1964, their fifth UK single, with Off the Hook on the B-side, per uDiscover Music.

Willie Dixon's blues, recorded on September 2, became the first blues record to top the UK chart on December 5, and the clip below is the original mono single.

The Rolling Stones play Moody Coliseum in Dallas

The Rolling Stones played Moody Coliseum at Southern Methodist University in Dallas on November 13, 1969, with Terry Reid and Chuck Berry on the bill, per the Wikipedia tour page.

The Rolling Stones Data setlist runs from Jumpin' Jack Flash to Street Fighting Man, and the clip below is Jumpin' Jack Flash.

Crosby, Stills, Nash & Young open four nights at Winterland

Crosby, Stills, Nash & Young opened a four-night run at the Winterland Ballroom in San Francisco on November 13, 1969, with Cold Blood, Joy of Cooking and Lamb on the bill, per the Bill Graham poster.

Their first album as a quartet with Neil Young, Déjà Vu, followed in March 1970, and the clip below is Woodstock from it.

concert

The Who play the gym at SUNY New Paltz

The Who played Elting Gymnasium at SUNY New Paltz in New York on Thursday, November 13, 1969, for hundreds of students, per the campus paper.

The 27-song Tommy tour set opened with John Entwistle's Heaven and Hell, and the clip below is Pinball Wizard.

Yellow Submarine opens in New York

The animated Beatles film Yellow Submarine opened in the United States, in New York, on November 13, 1968, per the Beatles' official site.

It had premiered in London on July 17, 1968, and the clip below is the title song from the Beatles' 1966 album Revolver.

The Rolling Stones hold No. 1 with Get Off Of My Cloud

The Rolling Stones' Get Off Of My Cloud was at No. 1 on the Official Singles Chart for 11 to 17 November 1965, its third week on the chart, per Official Charts.

It stayed at No. 1 for three weeks in November, and the clip below is the single, released in the UK on October 22.

The Rolling Stones play the Washington Coliseum

The Rolling Stones played the Washington Coliseum on November 13, 1965, the night after Greensboro and before Knoxville, per the Wikipedia tour page.

The tour ran from October 29 to December 5 behind Out of Our Heads, and the clip below is (I Can't Get No) Satisfaction.

The Beatles play two shows in Plymouth

The Beatles played two shows at the ABC Cinema in Plymouth, Devon, on November 13, 1963, the 10th date of their autumn tour, per The Beatles Bible.

The standard ten-song set included She Loves You and Twist and Shout, and the clip below is She Loves You.

The Beatles play the Star-Club in Hamburg

The Beatles played the Star-Club in Hamburg on November 13, 1962, their 61st concert at the club, during their second residency there, per the Paul McCartney Project.

Love Me Do had been out for more than five weeks, and the clip below is that debut single.

The Beatles play the Cavern at lunchtime

The Beatles played a lunchtime show at the Cavern Club in Liverpool on Monday, November 13, 1961, their 77th concert at the club, per the Paul McCartney Project.

Brian Epstein had first seen them there four days earlier, and the clip below is My Bonnie.

1960 in 1960s music: the full year guide

culture

Sammy Davis Jr. marries May Britt

Sammy Davis Jr. married Swedish actress May Britt on November 13, 1960, with Reform Rabbi William M. Kramer officiating after Britt converted to Judaism, per Wikipedia.

Interracial marriage was still forbidden by law in 31 US states, and the clip below is Davis singing his 1962 hit What Kind of Fool Am I? on The Ed Sullivan Show.
