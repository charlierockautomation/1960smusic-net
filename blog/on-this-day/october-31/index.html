Apple releases the Beatles' Something / Come Together in the UK

Apple released the Beatles' double A-side single Something / Come Together (Apple R 5814) in the UK on October 31, 1969, per the Beatles Bible.

It peaked at No. 4 on the UK chart, and the clip below is Something.

MC5 record the second night of Kick Out the Jams in Detroit

MC5 recorded the second night of their live debut album Kick Out the Jams at the Grande Ballroom in Detroit on October 31, 1968, per Wikipedia.

Elektra released the album in February 1969 with Jac Holzman and Bruce Botnick producing, and the clip below is the title track.

1967 in 1960s music: the full year guide

The Stooges play their first show at a Halloween party

The Stooges, then billed as the Psychedelic Stooges, made their live debut at a Halloween party in Ann Arbor, Michigan, on October 31, 1967, per Wikipedia.

Their next live gig did not come until January 1968, and the clip below is I Wanna Be Your Dog from their 1969 debut album.

culture

Brian Jones is freed from prison on bail

Brian Jones of the Rolling Stones was released from Wormwood Scrubs on bail pending appeal on October 31, 1967, a day after his nine-month sentence for drug possession, per This Day in Music.

A London appeals court overturned the sentence on December 12, 1967, and the clip below is the Stones' 1967 single We Love You.

1966 in 1960s music: the full year guide

Adam Horovitz of the Beastie Boys is born

Adam Horovitz, who performs as Ad-Rock in the Beastie Boys, was born in South Orange, New Jersey, on October 31, 1966, per Apple TV.

The Beastie Boys were inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2012, and the clip below is their single Sabotage.

1964

1964 in 1960s music: the full year guide

The Supremes hit No. 1 with Baby Love

The Supremes' Baby Love reached No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 on the chart dated October 31, 1964, per Wikipedia.

It held the top spot for four chart weeks as the second of five straight Supremes No. 1s, and the clip below is the single.

chart

Barbra Streisand hits No. 1 on the Billboard 200 with People

Barbra Streisand's album People reached No. 1 on the Billboard 200 on the chart dated October 31, 1964, per Wikipedia.

It ended the Beatles' 14-week run at the top with A Hard Day's Night and stayed No. 1 for five weeks, and the clip below is the title song.

concert

The Beatles play the Gaumont in Ipswich

The Beatles played the Gaumont Cinema in Ipswich on October 31, 1964, the 18th date of their 1964 British tour, per the Beatles Bible.

It was their second and final visit to Ipswich, and the clip below is their 1964 single A Hard Day's Night.
