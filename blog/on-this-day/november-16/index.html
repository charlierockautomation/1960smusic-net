Electric Ladyland hits No. 1 on the album chart

The Jimi Hendrix Experience's Electric Ladyland reached No. 1 on the Billboard album chart dated November 16, 1968, per the Wikipedia list of Billboard 200 number-one albums of 1968.

It held the top spot for two weeks and was the group's only No. 1 album, per the Wikipedia album page, and the clip below is All Along the Watchtower.

The Rolling Stones play two shows at Chicago

The Rolling Stones played two shows at the International Amphitheatre in Chicago on November 16, 1969, the night after Champaign, per the Rolling Stones Data chronology.

Hey Jude spends its eighth week at No. 1

The Beatles' Hey Jude held No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated November 16, 1968, its eighth week on top of a nine-week run that began September 28, per the Wikipedia list of Hot 100 number ones of 1968.

The nine weeks tied the record for the longest unbroken Hot 100 stay at No. 1, set by Percy Faith's Theme from A Summer Place in 1960, and the clip below is the song.

Stevie Wonder releases Down to Earth

Stevie Wonder's sixth studio album, Down to Earth, was released on the Tamla label on November 16, 1966, per Wikipedia.

The first single, A Place in the Sun, came out on October 24, 1966, and the clip below is that song, one of the key Motown soul releases of the year.

The Rolling Stones play Nashville

The Rolling Stones played the Municipal Auditorium in Nashville on November 16, 1965, the night after Charlotte, per the Wikipedia tour page.

The Nashville Scene described a noisy 40-minute set that ended with Get Off of My Cloud, which had just hit No. 1, and the clip below is that song.

The Animals record Don't Let Me Be Misunderstood

The Animals recorded Don't Let Me Be Misunderstood on November 16, 1964, with Mickie Most producing, per Wikipedia.

It was released in the UK on January 29, 1965, and reached No. 3 in the UK and No. 15 on the US Hot 100, and the clip below is the song.

Nino Tempo and April Stevens hit No. 1 with Deep Purple

Nino Tempo and April Stevens' Deep Purple was No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated November 16, 1963, per Billboard.

The brother-and-sister duo revived a 1933 Peter DeRose composition and won the Grammy for Best Rock and Roll Record, per Wikipedia, and the clip below is the single.

concert

The Beatles play Bournemouth Winter Gardens

The Beatles played the Winter Gardens in Bournemouth on Saturday, November 16, 1963, the 13th stop of their autumn tour, per the Beatles Bible.

It was their only appearance at that venue, and Wikipedia notes CBS filmed the show, and the clip below is Twist and Shout, a fixture of the tour set.

The Beatles tape their second Friday Spectacular

The Beatles recorded their second appearance on Radio Luxembourg's The Friday Spectacular at EMI House in London on November 16, 1962, per the Beatles Bible.

The show aired on November 23 and played both sides of their debut single, Love Me Do and P.S. I Love You, and the clip below is Love Me Do.

Patsy Cline records I Fall to Pieces

Patsy Cline recorded I Fall to Pieces at Bradley Studios in Nashville on November 16, 1960, with Owen Bradley producing and the Jordanaires on backing vocals, per Wikipedia.

Released on January 30, 1961, it reached No. 1 on the Hot Country chart and No. 12 on the Hot 100, a landmark of the Nashville Sound, and the clip below is the song.
