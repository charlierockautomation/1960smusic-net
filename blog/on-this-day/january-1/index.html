Moon Mullican dies at 57

Moon Mullican, the Texas country and western singer and pianist nicknamed the King of the Hillbilly Piano Players, died in Beaumont, Texas, on January 1, 1967, according to the Museum of the Gulf Coast and Birthplace of Western Swing.

He had suffered a heart attack on New Year's Eve, and his tombstone carries the title of his 1950 hit I'll Sail My Ship Alone, which is the clip below from his Topic channel.

Simon & Garfunkel' The Sound of Silence jumped from No. 5 to No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated January 1, 1966, after entering the chart at No. 80 on November 20.

The Beatles play the Astoria Theatre on New Year's Day

The Beatles played the Astoria Theatre in Finsbury Park, London, on January 1, 1964, the seventh night of The Beatles Christmas Show, with Billy J. Kramer and the Dakotas, Cilla Black, the Fourmost and Tommy Quickly on the bill, per the Paul McCartney Project.

Their single I Want to Hold Your Hand was their latest release, and the clip below is that song from the band's Topic channel.

The Beach Boys record the backing track for Fun, Fun, Fun

The Beach Boys recorded Fun, Fun, Fun on New Year's Day 1964 at United Western Recorders in Hollywood, with drummer Hal Blaine and saxophonist Steve Douglas joining the band, according to uDiscover Music.

Vocals and overdubs followed on January 8 or 9, and the single came out on February 3, 1964, and reached No. 5 on the Hot 100, as the clip below from the band's Topic channel shows.

The Beatles audition for Decca Records and are turned down

The Beatles auditioned at Decca Studios in West Hampstead, London, on January 1, 1962, playing fifteen songs in just under an hour, according to the Paul McCartney Project.

Decca chose Brian Poole and the Tremeloes instead, per This Day in Music, and the clip below is Like Dreamers Do from the audition, from the band's Topic channel.

Frequently Asked Questions

What was No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated January 1, 1966?

Simon & Garfunkel's The Sound of Silence reached No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated January 1, 1966, jumping from No. 5 and holding the top spot for two weeks. The Beatles' We Can Work It Out rose from No. 11 to No. 2 the same week.

When did Top of the Pops first air?

Top of the Pops was first broadcast on BBC television on Wednesday, January 1, 1964, at 6:35 pm from Dickenson Road Studios in Manchester. Jimmy Savile hosted, and Dusty Springfield's I Only Want to Be with You was the first song performed.

When did the Beatles audition for Decca Records?

The Beatles auditioned at Decca Studios in West Hampstead, London, on January 1, 1962, playing fifteen songs in just under an hour. Decca chose Brian Poole and the Tremeloes instead.

When did the Beach Boys record Fun, Fun, Fun?

The Beach Boys recorded Fun, Fun, Fun on New Year's Day 1964 at United Western Recorders in Hollywood, with vocals and overdubs added on January 8 or 9. The single was released on February 3, 1964, and reached No. 5 on the Hot 100.

Where were the Beatles on January 1, 1964 and January 1, 1965?

On January 1, 1964, the Beatles played the Astoria Theatre in Finsbury Park, London, as part of The Beatles Christmas Show. On January 1, 1965, they played two shows at the Hammersmith Odeon in London, at 6:15 pm and 8:45 pm, as part of Another Beatles Christmas Show.

When did Moon Mullican die?

Moon Mullican, the Texas country and western singer and pianist known as the King of the Hillbilly Piano Players, died in Beaumont, Texas, on January 1, 1967, at the age of 57, after a heart attack on New Year's Eve.
