John Lennon returns his MBE to the Queen

John Lennon returned his MBE to Queen Elizabeth II on November 25, 1969, as a protest against the Vietnam war, per the Beatles Bible.

His letter also cited Britain's role in the Nigeria-Biafra war and the slide of his single Cold Turkey down the charts, per OnThisDay.com, and the clip below is Cold Turkey.

The Beatles' White Album is released in the US

Apple released the Beatles' double album The Beatles, known as the White Album, in the US on November 25, 1968, three days after its UK release, per Wikipedia.

It spent nine weeks at No. 1 on the Billboard 200, and the clip below is Back in the U.S.S.R., the album's opening track.

Incense and Peppermints tops the Hot 100

Strawberry Alarm Clock's Incense and Peppermints was No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated November 25, 1967, per Wikipedia.

The Jimi Hendrix Experience play their first UK show at the Bag O'Nails

The Jimi Hendrix Experience played the Bag O'Nails Club in London on November 25, 1966, their first UK live show, per This Day in Music.

The band played from the venue's DJ booth, and the clip below is Hey Joe, recorded a month earlier.

Harrods opens for a Beatles Christmas shopping trip

Harrods in London opened especially for the Beatles on the evening of November 25, 1965, so they could shop for Christmas, per the Beatles Bible.

The band's next single, Day Tripper, came out in December, and the clip below is that song.

The Seekers reach No. 1 in the UK with The Carnival Is Over

The Seekers' The Carnival Is Over reached No. 1 on the UK singles chart on November 25, 1965, their second chart-topper, per This Day in Music.

It stayed on top for three weeks, per Official Charts, and the clip below is a live 2013 farewell-tour performance.

Mark Lanegan is born

Mark Lanegan, later the lead singer of Screaming Trees, was born in Ellensburg, Washington, on November 25, 1964, per Wikipedia.

He joined the band as its drummer in 1984 before taking over vocals, and the clip below is their 1992 single Nearly Lost You.

The Beatles film at Granada Television in Manchester

The Beatles spent the afternoon of November 25, 1963 at the Granada studios in Manchester, filming for Late Scene Extra with comedian Ken Dodd, per the Beatles Bible.

They performed I Want to Hold Your Hand and This Boy, the footage aired on November 27, and the clip below is the first of those songs.
