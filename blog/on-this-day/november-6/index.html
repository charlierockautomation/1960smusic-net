The Rolling Stones hit No. 1 with Get Off of My Cloud

The Rolling Stones topped the Billboard Hot 100 on the chart dated November 6, 1965, with Get Off of My Cloud, for two weeks, per Wikipedia.

It followed (I Can't Get No) Satisfaction as their second American No. 1, and the clip below is the single.

Jazz pianist Clarence Williams dies in Queens

Clarence Williams, the pianist, songwriter and producer born in Plaquemine, Louisiana, died in Queens, New York on November 6, 1965, per Wikipedia.

He led Clarence Williams' Blue Five, and the clip below is the band playing Mean Blues.

The Beatles play two shows at the Gaumont in Southampton

The Beatles played two shows at the Gaumont Cinema in Southampton on November 6, 1964, at 6.15pm and 8.40pm, with Mary Wells on the bill, per the Beatles Bible.

It was the 23rd date of their autumn British tour and their third and final Southampton concert, and the clip below is the title song of their 1964 film A Hard Day's Night.

The Beatles play the Star-Club in Hamburg

The Beatles played the Star-Club in Hamburg on November 6, 1962, the sixth show of a 14-night second residency that ran from November 1 to 14, per the Paul McCartney Project.

Love Me Do had come out a month earlier, and the clip below is that single.

Jimmy Dean hits No. 1 with Big Bad John

Jimmy Dean topped the Billboard Hot 100 on the chart dated November 6, 1961, with Big Bad John, for five weeks, per the list of 1961 Hot 100 number ones.

It was his only Hot 100 No. 1 and earned him a Grammy for best country and western recording, and the clip below is the single.
