The Temptations top the Hot 100 with I Can't Get Next to You

The Temptations' I Can't Get Next to You reached No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 on the chart dated October 18, 1969, per Billboard.

Written by Norman Whitfield and Barrett Strong, it held the top spot for two weeks, and the clip below is the record, a hit of Motown psychedelic soul.

John Lennon and Yoko Ono are arrested for cannabis possession

Police led by Sergeant Norman Pilcher raided 34 Montagu Square in London at 11:30am and arrested John Lennon and Yoko Ono, per the Beatles Bible.

The flat belonged to Ringo Starr, and Lennon took sole responsibility and pleaded guilty on November 28, and the clip below is Revolution, the 1968 Beatles single.

The Beatles finish If I Needed Someone and record In My Life

In Studio Two at EMI Studios, the Beatles added George Harrison's lead vocal, his 12-string Rickenbacker and Ringo Starr's tambourine to If I Needed Someone, per the Beatles Bible.

They then rehearsed and recorded three takes of Lennon's In My Life, which was completed on October 22, and the clip below is If I Needed Someone.

Wynton Marsalis is born in New Orleans

Trumpeter and composer Wynton Marsalis was born in New Orleans, the second of six sons of pianist Ellis Marsalis Jr., per BlackPast.

He became artistic director of Jazz at Lincoln Center, and the clip below is Caravan, a Marsalis recording in the jazz tradition.

West Side Story opens at the Rivoli Theatre in New York

The film version of West Side Story began its roadshow engagement at the Rivoli Theatre in New York on October 18, 1961, per Cinema Treasures.

The Rivoli run lasted 77 weeks, and the clip below is Rita Moreno's America from the film's soundtrack, with music by Leonard Bernstein.
