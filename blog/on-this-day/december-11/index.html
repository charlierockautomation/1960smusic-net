Sam Cooke is shot and killed in Los Angeles

Soul singer Sam Cooke, aged 33, was fatally shot at the Hacienda Motel in South Central Los Angeles on December 11, 1964, by the motel's manager, Bertha Franklin, per Wikipedia.

A coroner's jury ruled the death justifiable homicide, though some family members and supporters dispute the official account, and the clip below is A Change Is Gonna Come, the song that became his civil rights anthem.

The Magic Christian, with Ringo Starr, premieres in London

The film The Magic Christian, Ringo Starr's second movie, had its world premiere at the Odeon Theatre in Kensington, London, on December 11, 1969, with Princess Margaret attending, per the Beatles Bible and Wikipedia.

Ringo co-starred with Peter Sellers, and the clip below is Badfinger's Come and Get It, the song Paul McCartney wrote and produced for the film.

concert

George Harrison joins Delaney & Bonnie in Copenhagen again

George Harrison played the Falkoner Theatre in Copenhagen with Delaney & Bonnie on December 11, 1969, per the Beatles Bible.

It was the second of three consecutive nights he joined the husband-and-wife act, and the clip below is their song Only You Know and I Know.

The Rolling Stones begin filming Rock and Roll Circus

Filming of The Rolling Stones Rock and Roll Circus began at Intertel Studio in Wembley, London, on December 11, 1968, with performances starting around 2 pm, per Wikipedia.

Setup delays pushed the last performances to almost 5 am, it was Brian Jones's final appearance with the Stones, and the clip below is the Who's A Quick One (While He's Away) from the show.

1965 in 1960s music: the full year guide

The Byrds hold No. 1 with Turn! Turn! Turn!

The Byrds' Turn! Turn! Turn! was No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated December 11, 1965, the second of its three weeks on top, per Wikipedia.

The Dave Clark Five's Over and Over took over on the chart dated December 25, and the clip below is the Byrds' folk rock single, a hit for the folk rock sound.

concert

The Beatles play the Astoria in Finsbury Park

The Beatles played the Astoria Cinema in Finsbury Park, London, on December 11, 1965, one of their final two London concerts, per the Beatles Bible.

The set was eleven songs, and George Harrison called it one of the most incredible shows they had done, and the clip below is We Can Work It Out, which was in the set.

The Beatles play La Scala Ballroom in Runcorn

The Beatles played the La Scala Ballroom in Runcorn, Cheshire, on December 11, 1962, per the Beatles Bible.

It was the second of just two Beatles shows in the town, 14 miles south of Liverpool, and the clip below is P.S. I Love You, the B-side of their first single Love Me Do.

1961 in 1960s music: the full year guide

chart

Please Mr. Postman gives Motown its first Hot 100 No. 1

The Marvelettes' Please Mr. Postman reached No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated December 11, 1961, for one week, replacing Jimmy Dean's Big Bad John, per Wikipedia and Wikipedia.

It was the first Motown single to top the chart, and it later became a Motown standard that the Beatles covered on With the Beatles, and the clip below is the original.

concert

The Beatles play their 61st lunchtime show at the Cavern

The Beatles played a lunchtime show at the Cavern Club in Liverpool on Monday, December 11, 1961, their 61st lunchtime set there and 94th Cavern appearance overall, per the Beatles Bible.

Lunchtime shows typically ran from noon to 2 pm, and the clip below is Some Other Guy, filmed at the Cavern in 1962.
