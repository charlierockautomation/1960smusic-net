The Beatles collect their MBEs at Buckingham Palace

The Beatles received their MBE medals from Queen Elizabeth II at Buckingham Palace on October 26, 1965, in an 11am ceremony where the Queen pinned the medals to their lapels, per the Beatles Bible.

About 4,000 fans were held back by police outside, and the clip below is Drive My Car from Rubber Soul, an album they were recording at the time.

The San Francisco International Pop Festival opens in Pleasanton

The two-day San Francisco International Pop Festival began at the Alameda County Fairgrounds in Pleasanton, California, on October 26, 1968, per concert listings.

Deep Purple, Eric Burdon and the Animals, Canned Heat, Iron Butterfly and Creedence Clearwater Revival were on the bill, and the clip below is Deep Purple's Hush, their 1968 single.

Keith Urban is born in Whangarei, New Zealand

Country singer, songwriter and guitarist Keith Urban was born on October 26, 1967, in Whangarei, Northland, New Zealand, per Wikipedia.

His single Somebody Like You spent six weeks at No. 1 after its 2002 release, and the clip below is that song.

Alma Cogan dies at 34

British singer Alma Cogan died on October 26, 1966, at age 34, at Middlesex Hospital in London, per Wikipedia.

She had a UK No. 1 with Dreamboat in 1955, and she was a close friend of Brian Epstein and Paul McCartney of the Beatles, and the clip below is Dreamboat.

The Beatles collect their MBEs at Buckingham Palace

The Beatles received their MBE medals from Queen Elizabeth II at Buckingham Palace on October 26, 1965, in an 11am ceremony where 189 people received awards, per the Beatles Bible.

Afterward they held a press conference at the Saville Theatre, and stereo mixes of Rubber Soul tracks including Drive My Car were made at Abbey Road the same day, and the clip below is that song.

The Beatles finish recording Beatles For Sale

The Beatles held the last recording session for Beatles For Sale at Studio Two, Abbey Road, on October 26, 1964, cutting Honey Don't in five takes and remaking What You're Doing, per the Beatles Bible.

Ringo Starr sang lead on Honey Don't, which the album needed for his vocal spot, and the clip below is that track.

The Beatles play two shows at Stockholm's Tennishallen

The Beatles played two concerts, at 5pm and 8pm, at the Kungliga Tennishallen in Stockholm on October 26, 1963, per the Beatles Bible.

They were second on the bill to Joey Dee and the Starliters on the second night of their Swedish tour, and the clip below is I Saw Her Standing There, one of the nine songs in their likely set.

birth

Natalie Merchant is born in Jamestown, New York

Singer-songwriter Natalie Merchant was born on October 26, 1963, in Jamestown, New York, per Wikipedia.

She joined 10,000 Maniacs in 1981 as lead vocalist and primary lyricist and left in 1993 to go solo, and the clip below is Like the Weather.

1962 in 1960s music: the full year guide

concert

The Beatles play their first shows in Preston

The Beatles played two shows at the Public Hall on Lune Street in Preston, Lancashire, on October 26, 1962, their first appearance in the town, per the Beatles Bible.

Mike Berry and The Outlaws headlined the Preston Grasshoppers Rugby Football Club dance, with tickets at six shillings, and the clip below is P.S. I Love You, the B-side of their debut single.
