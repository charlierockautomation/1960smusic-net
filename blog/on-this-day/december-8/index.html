Jimi Hendrix goes on trial in Toronto on drug charges

Jimi Hendrix went on trial in Toronto on December 8, 1969, on drug possession charges from his May 3 arrest at Toronto International Airport, per Wikipedia and the Toronto Star.

A jury acquitted him on December 10 after about eight hours of deliberation, and the clip below is Purple Haze.

industry

Ringo Starr re-records Octopus's Garden for a George Martin TV special

At EMI Studios in Abbey Road on December 8, 1969, producer George Martin and engineers Geoff Emerick and Phil McDonald recorded new parts of Octopus's Garden with Ringo Starr and session musicians, per the Beatles Bible.

It let Starr mime on the TV special With A Little Help From My Friends, because Musicians' Union rules banned lip-syncing on British television, and the clip below is the original Abbey Road track.

Graham Nash plays his last show with the Hollies at the London Palladium

Graham Nash played his final concert with the Hollies on December 8, 1968, a benefit at the London Palladium, according to Nash's own later accounts, including a 2018 profile.

Nash says he was in Los Angeles with David Crosby and Stephen Stills two days later, and the clip below is Bus Stop, the Hollies hit from 1966.

Parlophone releases the Magical Mystery Tour EP in the UK

Parlophone released the Beatles' Magical Mystery Tour double EP in the UK on December 8, 1967, per the Beatles Bible and OnThisDay.com.

It came out more than two weeks before the film's BBC premiere, per the Beatles Bible, and the clip below is the title track.

release

Decca releases Their Satanic Majesties Request in the UK

Decca issued the Rolling Stones' Their Satanic Majesties Request (TXS 103) in the UK on December 8, 1967, per Rolling Stones Data and Stones on Decca; sources differ on whether the US release on London Records was December 8 or 9.

It was the band's sixth British studio album, and the clip below is She's a Rainbow.

Sinéad O'Connor is born in Glenageary, Dublin

Singer Sinéad O'Connor was born in Glenageary, County Dublin, on December 8, 1966, per Wikipedia and Songfacts.

She went on to record Nothing Compares 2 U, and the clip below is her video for it.

birth

Geto Boys rapper Bushwick Bill is born in Kingston, Jamaica

Richard Shaw, later known as Bushwick Bill, was born in Kingston, Jamaica, on December 8, 1966, and raised in Bushwick, Brooklyn, per Wikipedia and Songfacts.

He later rapped with the Houston group Geto Boys and died on June 9, 2019, and the clip below is Mind Playing Tricks on Me.

The Beatles play two shows at the Gaumont in Sheffield

The Beatles played two shows at the Gaumont Cinema in Sheffield on December 8, 1965, their first visit to the venue, per the Beatles Bible.

George Harrison visits Ringo Starr in hospital

George Harrison visited Ringo Starr in University College Hospital in London on December 8, 1964, six days after Starr's tonsils were removed, per the Beatles Bible.
