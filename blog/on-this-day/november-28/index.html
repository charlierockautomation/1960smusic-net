London Records releases Let It Bleed in the US

London Records released the Rolling Stones' album Let It Bleed in the US on November 28, 1969, a week before Decca issued it in the UK on December 5, per Wikipedia.

Jimmy Miller produced it, it reached No. 1 in the UK and No. 3 on Billboard's Top LPs chart, and the clip below is the opening track, Gimme Shelter.

concert

The Rolling Stones play two shows at Madison Square Garden

The Rolling Stones played two shows at Madison Square Garden in New York on November 28, 1969, after a single show the night before, per Rolling Stones Data.

Material from the Garden nights fed the live album Get Yer Ya-Ya's Out!, and the clip below is Midnight Rambler from those shows.

1968 in 1960s music: the full year guide

John Lennon is fined £150 for cannabis possession

John Lennon pleaded guilty at Marylebone Magistrates' Court in London on November 28, 1968, and was fined £150 plus 20 guineas in costs, per the Beatles Bible.

He took sole responsibility to protect Yoko Ono and the court quashed the obstruction charge, and the clip below is Revolution, the Lennon-written single from that year.

1967 in 1960s music: the full year guide

The Beatles record Christmas Time (Is Here Again)

The Beatles recorded and mixed Christmas Time (Is Here Again) in Studio Three at EMI Studios on November 28, 1967, with George Martin producing and Victor Spinetti joining in, per the Beatles Bible and Wikipedia.

It closed their fifth fan club Christmas record, sent to UK fans only, and the clip below is Hello, Goodbye, the single they had just released.

The Beatles try a new arrangement of Strawberry Fields Forever

The Beatles returned to Studio Two at Abbey Road on November 28, 1966, and began again on Strawberry Fields Forever with a new arrangement that opened on a Mellotron intro, per the Wikipedia.

That take was judged good enough for mixdown and overdubs, and the clip below is the finished song.

The Rolling Stones play two shows at Chicago's Arie Crown Theater

The Rolling Stones played two shows at the Arie Crown Theater in McCormick Place, Chicago, on November 28, 1965, during their second American tour, per the tour listing on Wikipedia.

They were touring behind Get Off Of My Cloud, and the clip below is that single.

The Shangri-Las hit No. 1 with Leader of the Pack

Leader of the Pack by the Shangri-Las reached No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 in the chart dated November 28, 1964, per Wikipedia.

George "Shadow" Morton produced the Red Bird single, the BBC refused to play it, and the clip below is the record itself.

1963

1963 in 1960s music: the full year guide

concert

The Beatles play the ABC Cinema in Lincoln

The Beatles played the ABC Cinema in Lincoln on November 28, 1963, the 23rd date of their autumn UK tour and their only Lincoln show, per the Beatles Bible.

Ringo Starr cut rehearsal short to have an earache treated at a local hospital, and the clip below is She Loves You from the tour's 10-song set.

1962

1962 in 1960s music: the full year guide

concert

The Beatles play the Cavern and Lewis's 527 Club in one night

The Beatles played the Cavern Club in the evening and then a late "Young Idea Dance" on the top floor of Lewis's department store in Liverpool on November 28, 1962, per the Beatles Bible.

The store staff party had a jazz band headlining with the Beatles at the bottom of the bill, two days after they recorded Please Please Me, which is the clip below.

1961

1961 in 1960s music: the full year guide

concert

The Beatles play their last Merseyside Civil Service Club show

The Beatles played the Merseyside Civil Service Club on Lower Castle Street, Liverpool, on November 28, 1961, the last of four straight Tuesday shows there, per the Beatles Bible.

The members-only basement club let guests in for three shillings, and the clip below is Love Me Do, the single that started their record career less than a year later.

Elvis Presley hits No. 1 with Are You Lonesome Tonight?

Elvis Presley's Are You Lonesome Tonight? reached No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 in the chart dated November 28, 1960, per Wikipedia.

He recorded it at RCA Studio B in Nashville in April 1960, it held the top spot into January 1961, and the clip below is the record.
