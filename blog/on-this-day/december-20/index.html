Peter, Paul and Mary reach No. 1 with Leaving on a Jet Plane

Peter, Paul and Mary's Leaving on a Jet Plane reached No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated December 20, 1969, replacing Steam's Na Na Hey Hey Kiss Him Goodbye.

The John Denver song, cut for their 1967 LP Album 1700, became the folk trio's only Hot 100 No. 1, per Wikipedia, and the clip below is the group's official lyric video.

The Beatles add backing vocals to When I'm Sixty-Four

Wilson Pickett records 634-5789 at Stax

Wilson Pickett recorded 634-5789 (Soulsville, U.S.A.) at Stax Studios in Memphis on December 20, 1965, with Steve Cropper, Duck Dunn, Al Jackson Jr. and Isaac Hayes behind him, per MusicBrainz.

The Eddie Floyd and Steve Cropper song became a No. 1 R&B hit for the soul shouter in 1966, per Wikipedia, and the clip below is the original recording.

Memphis jug band star Charlie Burse dies at 64

Charlie Burse, the singer and guitarist known as "Uke" from the Memphis Jug Band, died of heart disease in Memphis on December 20, 1965, per his Wikipedia biography, citing Eagle and LeBlanc's Blues: A Regional Experience.

His jug band records with Will Shade were part of the blues revival that fed 1960s folk and rock, and the clip below is Oil It Up and Go by Charlie Burse and His Memphis Mudcats.

The Osmond Brothers make their TV debut with Andy Williams

The Osmond Brothers, Alan, Wayne, Merrill and Jay, first appeared on NBC's The Andy Williams Show on December 20, 1962, per Wikipedia's history of the show, citing the Complete Directory to Prime Time Network TV Shows.
