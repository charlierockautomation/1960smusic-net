King Crimson play their first US show

King Crimson played their first American concert in the refectory at Goddard College in Plainfield, Vermont, on October 29, 1969, per the DGM Live account of Ian McDonald's diary.

It opened a US tour that ran to December 14, and the clip below is 21st Century Schizoid Man from their debut album.

The Who release My Generation in the UK

Brunswick released the Who's single My Generation, backed with Shout and Shimmy, in the UK on October 29, 1965, per Wikipedia.

It peaked at No. 2 on the UK chart, and the clip below is the original mono mix.

The Rolling Stones open a North American tour in Montreal

The Rolling Stones opened a North American tour at the Montreal Forum on October 29, 1965, per Wikipedia.

The run continued to December 5 in support of Out of Our Heads, and the clip below is Satisfaction.

The Beatles close their Swedish tour in Eskilstuna

The Beatles played the sports hall in Eskilstuna, Sweden, on October 29, 1963, the last concert of their Swedish tour, per the Beatles Bible.

About 2,000 people attended, and the clip below is their 1963 single She Loves You.

The Beatles tape a second People and Places

The Beatles taped a second appearance on Granada Television's People and Places in Manchester on October 29, 1962, performing Love Me Do and A Taste of Honey, per the Beatles Bible.

It aired in the north of England on November 2, by which time the band was in Hamburg, and the clip below is Love Me Do.
