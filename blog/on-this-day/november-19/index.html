Hendrix and Pink Floyd play Coventry Theatre

The Jimi Hendrix Experience played Coventry Theatre on Sunday, November 19, 1967, on a package tour with Pink Floyd, The Move, Amen Corner and The Nice, per the Bradford Timeline.

The bill paired the Experience with Pink Floyd while Syd Barrett was still in the group, and the clip below is Purple Haze.

The Supremes reach No. 1 with You Keep Me Hangin' On

The Supremes had You Keep Me Hangin' On at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated November 19, 1966, the first of two weeks on top, per the Wikipedia list of Hot 100 number ones of 1966.

It replaced Johnny Rivers' Poor Side of Town, and the clip below is the Motown single itself.

The Kinks release Till the End of the Day

The Kinks released the single Till the End of the Day in the UK on Pye on November 19, 1965, backed with Where Have All the Good Times Gone, per Wikipedia.

Ray Davies wrote it, Shel Talmy produced it, and it reached No. 8 in the UK, and the clip below is the song.

The Supremes go to UK No. 1 with Baby Love

Baby Love by the Supremes began a two-week run at No. 1 on the UK Singles Chart in the week starting November 19, 1964, per the Wikipedia list of UK Singles Chart number ones of the 1960s.

It was the group's only UK chart-topper, and the clip below is the Motown single.

The Beatles play the Gaumont in Wolverhampton

The Beatles played the Gaumont Cinema in Wolverhampton on Tuesday, November 19, 1963, the 14th date of their autumn tour, per the Beatles Bible.

Their ten-song set ended with Twist and Shout and included She Loves You, which is the clip below.

1962 in 1960s music: the full year guide

concert

The Beatles play two ballrooms in one night

The Beatles played Smethwick Baths Ballroom and then the Adelphi Ballroom in West Bromwich on Monday, November 19, 1962, per the Beatles Bible.

Love Me Do was their current single, and the clip below is the song.

concert

The Paul Winter Sextet plays the White House East Room

The Paul Winter Sextet performed in the East Room of the White House on November 19, 1962, at First Lady Jacqueline Kennedy's invitation, per the White House Historical Association.

More than 200 guests attended as part of the Musical Programs for Youth by Youth series, and the clip below is Little Boat from the sextet's album Jazz Meets the Bossa Nova.

What happened in 1960s music on November 19?
