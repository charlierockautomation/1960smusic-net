George Harrison is deported from Hamburg

George Harrison, 17, was deported from Hamburg on November 21, 1960, after authorities found he was too young to work in a nightclub after midnight, per the Beatles Bible.

The Beatles played the Kaiserkeller without him for a few more nights, and the clip below is My Bonnie, which they recorded in Hamburg in 1961.

Yoko Ono has a miscarriage with John Lennon at her side

Yoko Ono lost the baby she was expecting with John Lennon on November 21, 1968, and Lennon stayed at her side at Queen Charlotte's Hospital in London, per the Beatles Bible.

The couple named the child John Ono Lennon II, and the clip below is Baby's Heartbeat, the foetal heartbeat recorded before the loss and released on their 1969 album Life with the Lions.

The Who play the Civic Auditorium in Fargo

The Who played the Civic Auditorium in Fargo, North Dakota, on November 21, 1967, two days after the Hollywood Bowl, per the band's official gig archive.

Happy Jack, their first US Top 40 hit, reached No. 24, and the clip below is the single.

The Rolling Stones play Fort Worth

The Rolling Stones played the Will Rogers Memorial Center in Fort Worth, Texas, on November 21, 1965, the night after Shreveport, per Wikipedia.

The US tour ran from October 29 to December 5 in support of Out of Our Heads, and the clip below is Get Off of My Cloud.

Björk is born in Reykjavík

Björk Guðmundsdóttir was born in Reykjavík, Iceland, on November 21, 1965, per Wikipedia.

She became lead singer of the Sugarcubes before going solo in 1993, and the clip below is Human Behaviour, the lead single from her debut album Debut.

The Beatles play the ABC Cinema in Carlisle

The Beatles played the ABC Cinema in Carlisle on Thursday, November 21, 1963, the 17th date of their autumn tour and their second show there after February 8, per the Beatles Bible.

The 10-song set ended with Twist and Shout, one day before With the Beatles came out in the UK, and the clip below is All My Loving from that album.

The Beatles play two shows at the Cavern Club

The Beatles played a lunchtime and an evening show at the Cavern Club in Liverpool on Wednesday, November 21, 1962, their 145th lunchtime and 113th evening performance there, per the Beatles Bible.

Love Me Do was their current single, and the clip below is the song.
