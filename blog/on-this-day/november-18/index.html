Frequently Asked Questions

What happened in 1960s music on November 18?

November 18 saw 9 verified music events across seven years, including the Jimi Hendrix Experience and Pink Floyd at the Liverpool Empire in 1967, the Kinks releasing Dead End Street in 1966, and two Beatles shows at the Cavern Club.

When was Dead End Street by The Kinks released?

The Kinks released Dead End Street in the UK on Pye on November 18, 1966, with Big Black Smoke on the B-side. Ray Davies wrote it, Shel Talmy produced it and it peaked at No. 5 on the UK Singles chart.

Who played with Jimi Hendrix at the Liverpool Empire on November 18, 1967?

The Jimi Hendrix Experience played the Liverpool Empire on the autumn package tour alongside Pink Floyd, The Move, Amen Corner and The Nice, per the Bradford Timeline. The tour had played Sheffield the night before.

Did the Beatles play the Cavern Club on November 18?

Yes, in two years. The Beatles played evening shows at the Cavern Club in Liverpool on November 18, 1961 and November 18, 1962, per the Paul McCartney Project. The 1962 show came the night after their Matrix Hall date in Coventry.

Which song was No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 dated November 18, 1967?

Lulu's To Sir With Love was No. 1 on the chart dated November 18, 1967, its fifth and final week on top. Strawberry Alarm Clock's Incense and Peppermints replaced it the following week.

Which musicians were born on November 18?

Kim Wilde was born in Chiswick on November 18, 1960, and Kirk Hammett in San Francisco on November 18, 1962. Tim DeLaughter was born in Dallas on November 18, 1965, and Duncan Sheik in Glen Ridge, New Jersey, on November 18, 1969.
