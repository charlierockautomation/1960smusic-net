The Beatles finish Rubber Soul with an all-night session

The Beatles started the last recording session for Rubber Soul at 6pm on November 11, 1965 at EMI, Abbey Road, cutting You Won't See Me and Girl in two takes each, per the Paul McCartney Project.

They also added overdubs to Wait and I'm Looking Through You, and the clip below is You Won't See Me.

Jim Morrison is arrested after a flight to Phoenix

The Doors singer Jim Morrison was arrested in Phoenix on November 11, 1969, charged with drunk and disorderly conduct and interfering with the flight of an aircraft, per Ultimate Classic Rock.

He was flying to see the Rolling Stones at Veterans Coliseum and did not serve any time, and the clip below is Roadhouse Blues, a track from the Morrison Hotel sessions the band was working on that month.

John Lennon and Yoko Ono release Two Virgins in the US

Unfinished Music No. 1: Two Virgins by John Lennon and Yoko Ono came out in the US on Apple on November 11, 1968, with Tetragrammaton handling distribution, per the Beatles Bible.

The nude cover was hidden in a brown paper sleeve, and the clip below is Hey Jude, the Beatles single Apple had released that August.

The Beatles play the Star-Club in Hamburg

The Beatles played the Star-Club in Hamburg on Sunday, November 11, 1962, the 59th show the band had played at the club and part of their second residency there, per the Paul McCartney Project.

Love Me Do had come out five weeks earlier, and the clip below is that single.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happened in 1960s music on November 11?

November 11 saw 4 verified music events across four years, including the Beatles playing Hamburg in 1962, the Beatles finishing Rubber Soul in 1965, and Jim Morrison's arrest in 1969.

What did the Beatles record on November 11, 1965?

The Beatles cut You Won't See Me and Girl in two takes each at Abbey Road and added overdubs to Wait and I'm Looking Through You. It was the last recording session for Rubber Soul.

When was Two Virgins released in the US?

John Lennon and Yoko Ono's Two Virgins was released in the US on Apple on November 11, 1968, distributed by Tetragrammaton. The UK release followed later that month.

Why was Jim Morrison arrested on November 11, 1969?

Morrison was arrested in Phoenix and charged with drunk and disorderly conduct and interfering with the flight of an aircraft. He was flying to see the Rolling Stones and did not serve any time.

Where did the Beatles play on November 11, 1962?

The Beatles played the Star-Club in Hamburg, Germany, during their second residency there. It was the 59th show they had played at the club.
