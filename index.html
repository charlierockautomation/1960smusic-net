The definitive resource for the music that defined a decade.

Welcome to 1960smusic.net — a resource being built to celebrate the extraordinary music of the 1960s.

From the arrival of the British Invasion to the Motown hit factory, the West-Coast psychedelic explosion, the folk-rock awakening, surf and garage energy, Nashville and Bakersfield country, sophisticated pop and cool jazz, the 1960s reshaped popular music in barely ten years. Our goal is to map that decade in depth: genuine, fact-checked writing paired with tools that let you actually listen and explore, not just read.

The site is organized around eight core genres and hundreds of essential songs and artists. Everything here is researched from verifiable sources — we do not invent history, quotes or trivia. As sections are completed and reviewed, they will go live below.
