Random 60s Song Generator

Not sure what to listen to? Hit generate and let the decade pick for you.

About the random 60s song generator

The Random 60s Song Generator picks one song from the site's catalogue of nearly 200 records and plays it on the page.

You see the title, the artist, the year and a few mood tags, with the video right below.

How to use it

  • Leave the genre on All genres to draw from the whole decade.
  • Choose a genre to stay inside one sound, such as garage and surf rock or Motown and soul.
  • Press Generate another for a new song, and the same song never shows twice in a row.

It works well as a way to break out of a rut, since the catalogue covers hits and lesser-known records from all eight genres.

Where to go next

When a song catches your ear, many of them have a full story page, and the best 60s songs list shows which.

To hear more of the same sound, tune the matching station on the Radio Dial.

To explore a whole year instead, start at the 1960s hub.

The generator is free, needs no sign-up, and runs in your browser.

Each result shows mood tags taken from the song record, which gives a quick sense of what kind of listen it will be.

Try it a dozen times in a row and see how many artists you already know.
