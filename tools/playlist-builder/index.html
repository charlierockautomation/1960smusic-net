About the 60s party playlist builder

The Party Playlist Builder makes a 60s playlist for whatever mood the room is in.

Choose a vibe and a length of 6, 10 or 15 songs, and it pulls a shuffled set of 1960s tracks that match.

One player on the page plays the whole playlist, moving to the next song on its own when each one ends.

The eight vibes

  • Upbeat & Feel-Good
  • Romantic & Tender
  • Dance Party
  • Driving Rock
  • Dreamy & Psychedelic
  • Mellow & Reflective
  • Smooth & Sophisticated
  • Anthemic & Epic

Vibes are matched to mood tags on each song, not to genre, so one playlist can mix British Invasion, soul and pop.

Tips for a 60s party

Press Build again for a different mix with the same vibe.

Pick Dance Party early in the night and Mellow & Reflective near the end.

For a fixed list instead of a random one, use the 100 best 60s songs, or let the Radio Dial run in the background.

To plan around a single year, browse the 1960s year guides.

The builder is free, runs in your browser, and needs no sign-up.

Nothing is saved, so if a mix is a keeper, note the titles before you press Build again.

The songs come from the same catalogue as the rest of the site, so the artists range from household names to one-hit wonders.
