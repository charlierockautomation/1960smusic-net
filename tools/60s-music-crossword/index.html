FAQ

How do I play this 1960s music crossword?

Click any white square to select it, or click a clue in the Across or Down list to jump straight to that word.
Type letters to fill the grid, use the arrow keys to move around, and press Backspace to clear a square.
On a phone or tablet, tapping a square opens your device's keyboard automatically.

What if I get stuck on a clue?

Press Check to mark any incorrect letters in red without giving away the answer.
Press Reveal if you would rather see the completed grid.

Where do the crossword answers come from?

Every answer and clue is pulled from the same hand-checked catalogue of 1960s songs, artists and genres used across the rest of 1960smusic.net.
The 55 answers span all eight genre stations, from British Invasion bands to jazz and easy listening.

Is the 1960smusic.net crossword free to play?

Yes.
It runs right in your browser with no sign-up and no download, the same as every other tool on the site.

Can I play the crossword again?

Yes.
Reload the page for a fresh, empty grid and start again whenever you like.
