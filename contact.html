Contact

We'd love to hear from you.

1960smusic.net is currently in active development, but the inbox is open. Whether you've spotted an error, have a question, want to suggest an artist or song we should cover, or would like to discuss licensing or other business, please get in touch.

Email

The best way to reach us is by email:

contact@1960smusic.net

Response times. Because the site is still being built out, replies may take a little longer than usual — please allow up to a few business days. We read every message and particularly welcome factual corrections, which we prioritize.

Corrections

Accuracy matters to us. If you believe something on the site is incorrect, email us with the page and the specific detail in question, along with a source if you have one, and we'll review it promptly.

A note on what we can't do

We're a small, independent resource. We can't provide copies of recordings, help locate rare records for sale, or offer valuations. For listening, we link to official sources such as YouTube where available.
