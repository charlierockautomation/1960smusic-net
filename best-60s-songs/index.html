What Set 60s Songs Apart

Records Grew Longer and Stranger

The Animals' House of the Rising Sun broke the habit of keeping singles under three minutes.
Bob Dylan's Like a Rolling Stone ran six minutes, and the album cut of In-A-Gadda-Da-Vida passes 17.
The Beach Boys built Good Vibrations from many recording sessions.

New Sounds Entered Pop

Brian Jones played sitar on Paint It Black.
The Yardbirds put a harpsichord on a rock single in For Your Love.
The Doors used a Moog synthesizer on Strange Days, when the instrument was still new.

Songs Carried the Moment

Dylan's Blowin' in the Wind became a civil-rights anthem.
Aretha Franklin's Respect added the spelled-out hook that Otis Redding's original lacked.
Merle Haggard's Okie from Muskogee became an anthem amid the cultural divisions of the late decade.
