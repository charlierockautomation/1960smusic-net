1960s Music, Year by Year

Ten years, one decade, one page each.
Every year guide below covers that year's Billboard number ones, key albums and genre shifts, with a playable tracklist right on the page.

Year Guide

1960

19 Billboard number ones and Elvis Presley's record year.

 Year Guide

1961

Motown's first number one and Patsy Cline's Nashville Sound breakthrough.

 Year Guide

1962

The Twist's historic return to number one and Ray Charles's Nashville crossover.

 Year Guide

1963

Beatlemania sweeps Britain while Surfin' U.S.A. tops the year-end chart without ever hitting No. 1.

 Year Guide

1964

The Beatles hold all five top Hot 100 spots on April 4 and take six number ones in a year Beatlemania conquers America.

 Year Guide

1965

Wooly Bully tops the year-end chart without ever reaching number one, while the Beatles take five number ones and folk rock arrives.

 Year Guide

1966

The Ballad of the Green Berets tops the revised year-end chart, and 27 songs reach number one as folk rock and psychedelia arrive.

 Year Guide

1967

Lulu's To Sir With Love tops the year-end chart, and 19 songs reach number one as psychedelia and soul fill the Hot 100.

 Year Guide

1968

The Beatles' Hey Jude tops the year-end chart after nine weeks at number one, and Otis Redding scores the first posthumous number one.

 Year Guide

1969

The Archies' Sugar, Sugar tops the year-end chart, and the 5th Dimension spends nine weeks at number one.
